# Expert Secrets: The Perfect Webinar — Guided Skill

This skill walks the user through building a **complete, sellable presentation** — from offer to open to close — using the Perfect Webinar framework. Each secret produces a ready-to-use artifact that stacks into a full script.

1. Ask the user which secret they want to work on — OR walk through all five in sequence.

## SECRET #11 — THE STACK SLIDE

**Core concept:** Before anything else, build the irresistible offer. The goal is to show 10x the value of what you're charging. Stack everything the customer gets, assign a value to each element, total it up, then reveal the actual price.

**The 6 Elements of a Stack Slide:**

| # | Element | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Opportunity Switch Masterclass | The core system / course (the main thing they're buying) |
| 2 | The Tools | Templates, scripts, checklists, software — things that make it easier |
| 3 | Tangible #1 — Vehicle Belief | Overcomes false beliefs about the NEW OPPORTUNITY itself |
| 4 | Tangible #2 — Internal Belief | Overcomes false beliefs about THEMSELVES (I can't do this) |
| 5 | Tangible #3 — External Belief | Overcomes outside obstacles (time, money, economy, tech) |
| 6 | Exclusive Bonus | Creates urgency and scarcity (time-limited, quantity-limited, or access-based) |

**Value Rule:** If selling at $97 → stack total should reach at least $997. If selling at $997 → stack total should reach at least $9,997. Values represent what they GET from each element, not what you'd sell it for separately.

**Intake questions:**
1. What is your core offer — what's the main thing they're learning/getting?
2. What's the price point you're selling at?
3. What tools, templates, scripts, or shortcuts can you include?
4. What do people NOT believe about your vehicle? (What tangible can address this?)
5. What do people NOT believe about THEMSELVES? (What tangible addresses this?)
6. What OUTSIDE force do they blame for their inability to succeed? (What addresses this?)
7. What can you offer as a time/quantity-limited exclusive bonus?

**Artifact to generate:** A formatted Stack Slide breakdown with all 6 elements listed, individual values assigned, running total, and final price reveal. Includes the "10x value check."

## SECRET #12 — THE PERFECT WEBINAR (OVERVIEW)

**Core concept:** The entire presentation has ONE goal — get them to believe ONE thing (the Big Domino). Everything else exists to knock down that domino from multiple angles.

**The 4-Part Structure:**

| Part | Name | Goal | Time |
|------|------|------|------|
| 1 | Introduction / Building Rapport | Welcome, build rapport, pique curiosity, start persuading | 5–10 min |
| 2 | The One Thing | Introduce the Big Domino via Epiphany Bridge origin story | 10–15 min |
| 3 | The 3 Secrets — Breaking & Rebuilding Belief Patterns | Break the 3 false beliefs (vehicle, internal, external) through story | 45–60 min |
| 4 | The Stack + Close | Transition to offer, present the stack, use proven closes | 10–20 min |

**The 3 Secrets are NOT new beliefs.** They are the three false beliefs holding people back from believing the Big Domino. When all three fall, the Big Domino falls with them.

**Intake questions:**
1. What is your Big Domino statement? (From Secret #5 — or build it now)
2. What is the title of your webinar? (Format: "How to [result] without [obstacle]")
3. What are the 3 false beliefs most people have about: (a) your vehicle, (b) themselves using it, (c) outside obstacles?
4. What are the titles of your 3 Secrets? (These attack the 3 false beliefs above)

**Artifact to generate:** A Perfect Webinar Master Blueprint — one-page overview with the Big Domino, webinar title, 3 Secrets named, and structure timeline. The roadmap for the whole presentation.

**See:** `references/secret-12-perfect-webinar-overview.md`

## SECRET #13 — THE ONE THING (INTRODUCTION SCRIPT)

**Core concept:** The first 5–10 minutes. Build rapport instantly, set the ruler (their expectation), introduce the Big Domino, and tell your origin Epiphany Bridge story. If they believe your Big Domino by slide 6, everything after only reinforces it.

**The 9 Slides of the Introduction:**

| # | Slide | What You Say / Do |
|---|-------|------------------|
| 1 | Title Slide | "Welcome to [webinar]. Today I'm going to show you how to [result] without [obstacle]." |
| 2 | Intro / Rapport | Use Blair Warren's 5: Justify failures → Allay fears → Throw rocks at enemies → Confirm suspicions → Encourage dreams |
| 3 | The Ruler: Goal #1 | State the goal — the new opportunity + inclusion statement (beginners AND advanced) |
| 4 | The Ruler: Goal #2 | State the Big Domino as a goal sentence |
| 5 | Qualify Yourself | Briefly show external result + internal result. NOT a 10-page bio. |
| 6 | Future-Based Cause | Paint the vision of what their life looks like after they adopt the new opportunity |
| 7 | The One Thing | "There is ONE thing... if you believe this ONE thing, you will [desired outcome]." Introduce the Big Domino directly. |
| 8 | Epiphany Bridge Story #1 | Tell your origin story using the Epiphany Bridge Script |
| 9 | Liken + Case Study | Make it relevant to them. Reframe for their situation. Quick social proof. |

**Blair Warren's 5-Point Rapport Formula (Slide 2):**
- **Justify failures:** "If you've failed at X before, it's not your fault…"
- **Allay fears:** "If you've been concerned you can't succeed…"
- **Throw rocks at enemies:** "The [industry/gatekeepers] want you to think you need [X]…"
- **Confirm suspicions:** "If you've ever thought [common suspicion], you're right…"
- **Encourage dreams:** "I know you have a dream to [aspiration]…"

**Intake questions:**
1. Who has failed your audience before, and why? (Justify their failures)
2. What do they fear most about starting? (Allay their fears)
3. Who is the common enemy holding them back? (Throw rocks)
4. What do they secretly suspect is true about why they're stuck? (Confirm suspicions)
5. What is their big dream / vision of success? (Encourage dreams)
6. How do you qualify yourself? (One external result + one internal transformation)

**Artifact to generate:** A fully scripted Introduction section — slides 1–9 — written word for word in the user's voice, with all 5 Blair Warren rapport elements, Big Domino reveal, and Epiphany Bridge origin story placeholder.

**See:** `references/secret-13-one-thing-intro.md`

## SECRET #14 — BREAKING AND REBUILDING BELIEF PATTERNS

**Core concept:** This is NOT a teaching section. This is a belief-shifting section. You are not giving them information — you are destroying the beliefs that are keeping them stuck and installing new ones. Teaching what to do KILLS sales. Changing how they SEE THE WORLD causes people to buy.

> "The greatest service you can provide for someone is getting them to buy something. The act of buying creates a commitment that causes them to actually take action."

**The 15 Steps — Repeated 3 Times (once per Secret):**

For each of the 3 Secrets, run through these 5 steps:

| Step | What You Do |
|------|-------------|
| 10/15/20 | **State the Secret** — Name it. One sentence. Then: "Let me tell you a quick story…" |
| 11/16/21 | **Share Your Epiphany Bridge** — The story that breaks the false belief and installs the new one |
| 12/17/22 | **Show Other People's Results** — Testimonials from people JUST LIKE them (not superheroes) |
| 13/18/23 | **Break the Related Beliefs** — Jason Fladlien technique: "You're probably thinking X, right? Well actually…" |
| 14/19/24 | **Restate as Undeniable Truth** — The old belief is gone. Install the new belief as fact. |

**Step 25 — The Transition to Selling:**
Ask the recap question: "If you followed Secret #1 and [X], then Secret #2 and [Y], then Secret #3 and [Z]... do you think you could be successful?"
When they nod yes — the Big Domino has fallen.

**Step 26 — The Question (Armand Morin's Magic Sentence):**
"Let me ask you a question…"
→ "How many of you are excited about what we just talked about?"
→ "How many of you are feeling a little overwhelmed?"
→ [Fire hose joke / laugh]
→ **Ask permission:** "Is it okay with you if I spend 10 minutes going over a very special offer I created to help you implement __________?"
Wait for yes. Once you get permission, the close begins.

**Intake questions — run per Secret:**
1. What is Secret #1 title? (The vehicle false belief)
2. What Epiphany Bridge story breaks that belief?
3. What testimonials/results support it?
4. What are the 3–5 "you're probably thinking X" objections for this secret?
5. Repeat for Secret #2 (internal) and Secret #3 (external)

**Artifact to generate:** A fully scripted content section — steps 10–26 — for all 3 Secrets, with Epiphany Bridge story outlines, objection-busting scripts, and transition language. Written conversationally, belief-shift framing throughout.

## SECRET #15 — THE STACK

**Core concept:** The Stack is the close. Instead of revealing the offer all at once, you build it piece by piece — adding one element at a time and re-anchoring the full accumulated value each time before moving to the next item. By the time you reveal the price, they've already said yes in their head a dozen times.

**How the Stack Works:**
1. Introduce Element #1 → State its value → Show the stack so far ($XXX)
2. Add Element #2 → State its value → Show the running stack ($XXX + $XXX = $XXXX)
3. Continue adding each element → Restate EVERYTHING each time
4. Before revealing price: "If you got JUST [Element #1] for what I'm about to charge, it would be worth it. But you're also getting [2], [3], [4], [5], and [6]..."
5. The Price Anchor: "You might expect to pay $X,XXX for this. But today..."
6. Reveal the price

**Proven Closes to Weave In:**

| Close | What It Does |
|-------|-------------|
| **The If/Then Close** | "If [desired outcome] is something you want, then this is for you." |
| **The Reluctant Hero Close** | "I almost didn't offer this because [reason], but I want to give you every chance to succeed." |
| **The Who This Is For** | Describe your perfect buyer clearly. They self-identify. |
| **The Warning** | "Here's what happens if you leave today without this..." |
| **The Testimonial Close** | Last-minute social proof from someone just like them |
| **The Urgency/Scarcity** | Why they need to decide NOW (exclusive bonus expires, spots limited, etc.) |
| **The Guarantee** | Remove all risk. Flip the risk entirely onto you. |
| **The Price Is Peanuts** | Compare price to cost of NOT solving the problem |

**Intake questions:**
1. What is your actual price point?
2. What's the "expected" price you can anchor against?
3. Walk through the stack (use outputs from Secret #11)
4. What closes are most relevant for their audience?
5. What is the guarantee?
6. What is the call to action URL/step?

**Artifact to generate:** A fully scripted Stack close — element-by-element reveal script with running value totals, price anchor, selected closes woven in, guarantee statement, and final CTA.

## CRITICAL REMINDERS WHEN COACHING THE USER

- **Teaching kills sales.** If they want to add more tactics and how-to, redirect: "Save that for inside the course."
- **One belief only.** If they're trying to make multiple points, remind them: more than one belief = conversions drop by half.
- **The 3 Secrets are not new ideas.** They are angles on the ONE Big Domino. Reframe until they understand this.
- **Buying = commitment.** Help users feel good about selling hard. The act of buying is what creates transformation, not free content.
- **No technobabble.** Third-grade level throughout. "Kinda like" bridges for anything complex.
