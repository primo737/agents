# Secret #11 — The Stack Slide (Deep Reference)

## Purpose
Build the irresistible offer BEFORE writing a single word of the presentation. The offer is the destination. The whole webinar exists to get people excited enough to buy what you've already built here.

## The 10x Value Rule
- Selling at $97 → stack total must reach $970+
- Selling at $997 → stack total must reach $9,970+
- Selling at $9,997 → stack total must reach $99,970+

Value = what the results from each element are worth to them, not what you'd sell it for separately.

## The 6 Elements

### Element #1: Opportunity Switch Masterclass
The core training. The main thing they're buying. This is your beta masterclass recording, your course, your program. This is what teaches them the new opportunity.

*Assign value: What would it be worth to hire a private coach to teach them this?*

### Element #2: The Tools
Templates, scripts, checklists, contracts, software, cheat sheets, timelines, done-for-you assets.

**Key insight:** "People want tangible assets that make the core training easier to implement." Extra training = more work. Tools = easier success.

Examples:
- Liz Benny gives away her social media manager contracts (a by-product of her own business)
- Swipe files, email templates, funnel templates
- Checklists and implementation guides

*Assign value: What would it cost to hire someone to create these from scratch?*

### Element #3: Tangible #1 — Vehicle Belief Breaker
What false belief do people have about the VEHICLE itself (the new opportunity)?
Create something that shows proof the vehicle works:
- Case study booklet
- "20 examples of this working" video
- Results compilation

*Assign value: What would this proof be worth if they had to find it themselves?*

### Element #4: Tangible #2 — Internal Belief Breaker
"That's cool, but I don't think I can do it."
What do they not believe about THEMSELVES?
Create something that removes the "I can't" belief:
- Tech training for non-technical people
- Copywriting templates for non-writers
- Done-for-you components for people who freeze at implementation

*Assign value: What would hiring someone to do this for them cost?*

### Element #5: Tangible #3 — External Belief Breaker
"That might work, but [outside force] will stop me."
What OUTSIDE obstacle do they use as an excuse?
- Traffic/attention training
- Time-management guide
- Economy/budget workaround
- Accountability system

*Assign value: What would solving this external problem save them in time or money?*

### Element #6: Exclusive Bonus
Creates urgency + scarcity. Only available to those who act before [deadline/quantity].
Options:
- Personal access (consult call, accountability group, mastermind seat)
- Upgraded product tier (e.g., upgraded account for X months)
- First 10/20/50 get [something special]
- Bonus that expires at a specific date/time

*Assign value: What would it cost to get this access any other way?*

# Secret #12 — The Perfect Webinar Overview (Deep Reference)

## The ONE Goal
**Get them to believe ONE thing.**

Jason Fladlien: "The idea is to have a single point of belief that your message is built around and is emphasized over and over and over again from a variety of different angles."

The Big Domino is that belief. The 3 Secrets are not new beliefs — they are angles of attack on the same Big Domino.

## The 4 Parts at a Glance

```
PART 1 — INTRODUCTION (5–10 min)
Goal: Rapport, curiosity, excitement
Persuasion begins HERE, not at the close.

PART 2 — THE ONE THING (10–15 min)
Goal: Introduce the Big Domino
Tool: Epiphany Bridge origin story
First attempt to knock down the domino.

PART 3 — THE 3 SECRETS (45–60 min)
Goal: Break 3 false beliefs through story
Secret 1 → Vehicle belief
Secret 2 → Internal belief (I can't do this)
Secret 3 → External belief (X is stopping me)

PART 4 — THE STACK + CLOSE (10–20 min)
Goal: Present offer, get permission, stack and close
Tool: The Stack Slide + proven closes
```

## Where Everything From Expert Secrets Fits

| Framework | Where It Lives |
|-----------|---------------|
