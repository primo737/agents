# Secret #13 — The One Thing: Introduction Script (Deep Reference)

## Goal of the Introduction
Persuasion starts the MOMENT you start talking. By slide 9, they should already be nodding yes to the Big Domino — even before the "content" begins.

## Slide-by-Slide Breakdown

### Slide 1 — Title Slide
**Headline format:** "How to [desired result] without [biggest obstacle/fear]"

Script:
> "Hey everybody! Welcome to the webinar. This is [NAME] and today I'm going to show you how to [RESULT] without [OBSTACLE]."

Keep it clean. Curiosity + promise. Nothing more.

---

### Slide 2 — Intro / Rapport (Blair Warren's 5)

**The formula — hit all 5 in 2–3 minutes:**

1. **Justify their failures**
> "Now I'm guessing for a lot of you this is probably not your first [webinar / attempt at X]. If you've tried [X] before and it didn't work, I want you to know — it's not your fault. There's a lot of [confusing / bad / contradictory] information out there..."

2. **Allay their fears**
> "If you've been worried that you just can't [succeed / figure this out / make it work for you] — I want to put those fears to rest right now. You can do this. You just need the right [system / person / approach] to make it click."

3. **Throw rocks at their enemies**
> "The [industry / gurus / corporations] want you to believe you need [expensive thing / credential / special access] to [get result]. I'm here to tell you — that's not true. They have their own reasons for wanting you to think that..."

4. **Confirm their suspicions**
> "If you've ever suspected that [common belief about why they're stuck], you're probably right. The system isn't designed to help you win. That's why what I'm going to share today is different..."

5. **Encourage their dreams**
> "I know you have a dream to [specific aspiration]. I want to help you make that happen today."

---

### Slide 3 — The Ruler: Goal #1 (New Opportunity + Inclusion)
Tell them what they're going to get AND make it clear this is for them — both beginners AND more advanced people. This prevents the whole presentation being undermined by "is this for me?" doubt.

> "My goal for this presentation is to help two types of people.
> For those who are just getting started with [X], you're going to [specific beginner win].
> For those of you who already [have some experience], you're going to [specific advanced win]."

---

### Slide 4 — The Ruler: Goal #2 (Big Domino)
State the Big Domino as a goal statement. Not a pitch — a promise.

> "Specifically, what I want you to walk away believing today is that [NEW OPPORTUNITY] is the key to [DESIRED RESULT] — and that [VEHICLE] is the fastest, most reliable way to get there."

---

### Slide 5 — Qualify Yourself
Brief. External result first. Then internal transformation. NOT a long bio.

> "[External credential/result]. But more importantly, what that gave me was [internal result / feeling / freedom]."

Example:
> "I've helped over 40,000 entrepreneurs build sales funnels. But what I really care about is that I never have to miss my kids' games because of a speaking trip."

---

### Slide 6 — Future-Based Cause
Paint the vision. What does their life look like AFTER they adopt this opportunity?

> "I want to show you a world where [vivid description of their desired life after the transformation]. And it's more possible than you think right now."

---

### Slide 7 — The One Thing
Introduce the Big Domino directly. Make it feel like a revelation.

> "There is ONE thing — just one thing — that if you truly believe it, will change everything for you. And that one thing is: [BIG DOMINO]."

---

### Slide 8 — Epiphany Bridge Story #1 (Origin Story)
Tell the Epiphany Bridge story that first convinced YOU this new opportunity was real.
Use the 8-section script from Secret #8. Feelings-first. Third-grade level. No technobabble.

---

### Slide 9 — Liken to Them + Case Study
After your story, some people think: "That's great for you, but my situation is different."
Bridge it.

> "Now you might be thinking — 'I'm not [who Russell is], how does this apply to me?'
> Here's how it works for people in [their situation]: [reframe for their world].
> In fact, [NAME] was in exactly that situation, and here's what happened: [30-second story/result]."

---

# Secret #14 — Breaking and Rebuilding Belief Patterns (Deep Reference)

## The Core Principle
> "This is NOT a teaching presentation. This is a presentation to inspire people and cause them to take action."

Teaching gives them information. Belief-shifting changes how they see the world.
You can give someone all the information in the world and they'll leave and do nothing.
You break one belief that's been holding them back for years — they run to the back of the room.

## The 5-Step Loop (Run Once Per Secret)

### Step 1 — State the Secret
One sentence. Then immediately say: "Let me tell you a quick story…"

### Step 2 — Share Your Epiphany Bridge
Tell the story that breaks the specific false belief this secret addresses.
This is NOT an origin story — it's a story that makes this particular secret feel true.

### Step 3 — Show Other People's Results
Normal people. Not superheroes. People just like your audience.
> "The cool thing is it didn't just work for me. Here's [NAME], who always thought [false belief], but after [action], this is what happened: [result]."

### Step 4 — Break the Related Beliefs (Jason Fladlien Technique)
Go through every possible remaining objection tied to THIS secret.
Format: "You're probably thinking __________, right? Well actually, __________."

Run 3–8 of these. 30–60 seconds each. Short story or one-liner to bust each one.

Example:
> "You're probably thinking you need a lot of money to drive traffic, right? Well actually, you only need 100 clicks a day."
> "You're probably thinking you need to know how to code, right? Well actually, you can just clone other people's funnels right inside ClickFunnels."

### Step 5 — Restate as Undeniable Truth
> "So now you can see that [NEW BELIEF stated as fact]. The old idea that [OLD BELIEF] — that's gone. You now know that [TRUTH]."

---

## Step 25 — The Transition Question

After all 3 Secrets, run the recap question:

> "Let me ask you something. If you followed what I showed you in Secret #1 and [action/result], and then you did what I showed you in Secret #2 and [action/result], and then you used Secret #3 to [action/result]... do you think you could be successful?"

Watch for the nods. Heads nodding = Big Domino fell. If anyone ISN'T nodding, a belief somewhere wasn't shattered.

---

## Step 26 — The Question (Armand Morin's Magic Sentence)

> "Let me ask you a question…"

Then:
> "How many of you are excited about what we just talked about?!"
> "How many of you are feeling a little overwhelmed because we've covered so much?"
> [Fire hose joke — show a funny image to get a laugh]
> "I know I threw a lot at you, and it's impossible to cover everything you need in [X] minutes. That's why I created a special package for those who are ready to move forward..."

**Ask permission:**
> "Is it okay with you if I spend just 10 minutes going over a very special offer I created to help you implement [topic]?"

Wait for yes. Silence? Use:
> "It's totally fine if you don't want to know this stuff. I already know it. This isn't about me — this is for you. Are you okay if I spend 10 minutes showing you how it can change your life?"

# Secret #15 — The Stack (Deep Reference)

## Why the Stack Works
Most presenters reveal the offer all at once. By the time they finish listing everything, the audience has forgotten the first items.

The Stack reveals one element at a time — and every time you add something new, you show the FULL running total of what they're getting. They say yes in their head at every single element. By the time you reveal the price, they've already committed internally a dozen times.

## The Stack Sequence Script

**For each element:**
1. Name the element
2. Explain what it does and why it matters
3. Assign the value
4. Show the stack slide with everything so far + running total
5. "And if that were everything you got today, it would be worth [running total]. But wait..."

**Final stack reveal script:**
> "So let's recap everything you're getting today:
> You're getting [Element 1] — a $XXX value.
> You're also getting [Element 2] — a $XXX value.
> Plus [Element 3] — $XXX.
> Plus [Element 4] — $XXX.
> Plus [Element 5] — $XXX.
> AND you're getting [Element 6 — Exclusive Bonus] — a $XXX value — but ONLY if you sign up before [deadline/limit].
> That's a total value of $X,XXX.
> Now, you might expect to invest $X,XXX or even $X,XXX for everything here.
> But today, you're not going to pay $X,XXX. You're not even going to pay $X,XXX.
> Your total investment today is just $___."

## The Proven Closes

### 1. The If/Then Close
> "If [specific result they said they wanted] is something you truly want, then [PROGRAM NAME] is for you."

### 2. Who This Is For (Self-Identification)
Describe your perfect buyer in detail. They self-select in.
> "This is for you if: [specific situations]. This is NOT for you if [specific disqualifiers]."

### 3. The Warning
> "Here's what I've seen happen to people who leave today without getting started: [honest consequence]. And I don't want that for you."

### 4. The Reluctant Hero
> "Honestly, I almost didn't offer this today because [reason]. But I know too many people who needed exactly this and didn't have access to it, so I made the decision to..."

### 5. The Testimonial Close
One final story from a real customer — someone exactly like your audience — right before the price.

### 6. Urgency / Scarcity
Reference the exclusive bonus and the deadline/quantity limit.
> "Remember, [Exclusive Bonus] is only available to the first [X] people / before [DATE]. Once it's gone, it's gone."

### 7. The Guarantee
Flip all the risk onto yourself.
> "And here's my promise to you: if you go through [PROGRAM] and you don't [specific result], I'll [refund / work with you personally / give you X]. I'm taking on all the risk here because I know this works."

### 8. Price Is Peanuts
Compare the price to the cost of NOT solving the problem.
> "Think about what staying stuck is costing you right now. Every month you don't have [solution] is costing you [specific $ or opportunity]. What I'm offering today is literally [price] to solve a [much bigger] problem."

### 9. The Call to Action
Clear, direct, specific.
> "Go to [URL] right now. Click [button]. [1–2 step process]. I'll see you on the inside."
