## What Is an Authority Amplifier?

An Authority Amplifier is a free training, webinar, masterclass, or presentation that establishes credibility by delivering real value before asking for anything. It's not a sales pitch dressed as content — it's the opposite. It gives so much value that the audience *wants* to go deeper.

The formula: Teach the WHAT, not the HOW.
- WHAT = awareness (the problem, the insight, the framework)
- HOW = transformation (what your program delivers)

When you teach the WHAT for free and leave the HOW for your paid program, you've created a gap. That gap is the sales funnel.

---

## Phase 1: FRAMING — Opening and Credibility

### Title & Promise

The AA title follows the same formula as the MDM:
`"How to [achieve specific result] in [timeframe] — without [pain]"`

Or: `"The [Number] [Mistakes/Secrets/Steps] [Avatar Type] Need to Know About [Topic]"`

Ask: "What's the biggest misconception your avatar has that's keeping them stuck? That's your authority amplifier topic."

Draft 3 title options. Choose the one that promises the most specific, believable outcome.

### 3-Level Proof

Establish credibility in the first 2 minutes with three levels:

**Level 1 — Personal proof:** Your own results. "I went from [before] to [after]."

**Level 2 — Client proof:** Results you've gotten for others. "Here's what happened for [name]..."

**Level 3 — System proof:** Why your methodology works. "The reason this works is..."

Ask: "Give me one sentence for each level. Your story, one client story, and one reason your system is different."

---

## Phase 2: CONTENT — The Core Teaching

Structure the content around three problems your avatar has.

For each problem:
1. Name it clearly (in the avatar's language, not yours)
2. Show why most people get it wrong
3. Give the insight that shifts their thinking
4. Hint at the step in your program that solves it fully

**The Lightbulb Rule:** Every 7–10 minutes, the audience should have a new insight they didn't have before. If you go 15 minutes without giving them something new, you've lost them.

**The Curiosity Loop:** End each section with a question or tease that makes them want the next section. "And in a moment, I'm going to show you why most people solve Problem 2 in exactly the wrong order..."

Ask: "What are the 3 biggest problems your avatar faces on their journey to [MDM result]? For each one — what do most people think the solution is, and what's the real answer?"

---

## Phase 3: ACTION — The Close

The AA ends with a context-rich invitation to take the next step.

**Context (2 minutes):**
Explain who you are, who you work with, and how. Not a sales pitch — just orientation. "At this point, you know [what you've taught]. The people I work with are typically at [situation]. Here's how I can help..."

**The Single CTA:**
One action only. Not "follow me, buy this, and also check out..." — ONE thing.

Options:
- Book a call
- Apply for the program
- Join the waitlist
- Get the free resource (ULM)

Ask: "What's the ONE action you want someone to take after watching/attending your AA?"

**Objection Pre-frame:**
Before the CTA, address the top 2 objections your avatar has about taking that action. Handle them with stories, not arguments.

---

## Step-Specific Authority Amplifiers

Beyond the core AA, build 2 step-specific AAs tied to specific steps in the product roadmap. These are shorter (15–20 min vs. 45–60 min for the core AA) and target people at specific stages of awareness.

For each: pick a step from the roadmap that has a teachable insight, and build a mini-AA around it.
