# The Four Launch Sequences

## 1. Pre-Prelaunch Sequence (Pre-Pre)

**Duration**: 1-2 communications
**Purpose**: Warm up the market and gather intelligence

**Strategy**:
- Gain attention without being overly sales-oriented (the "shot across the bow")
- Test market interest
- Surface potential objections early
- Gather feedback to finalize the offer
- Make the process collaborative

**Tactics**:
- Top Two Questions Survey: Ask prospects about their biggest questions or challenges
- Simple social media posts teasing upcoming content
- Brief emails building curiosity
- Start segmenting and tagging your list based on responses

**Key Outcome**: Intelligence gathering and setting the stage for Prelaunch

---

## 2. Prelaunch Sequence (The Heart and Soul)

**Duration**: Typically 7-10 days (can range from 3-27 days)
**Purpose**: Deliver massive value and build relationships before asking for a sale

### The Three-Act Structure:

### PLC #1: Opportunity (The Journey)

**Core Question**: "Why should they care?"
**Focus**: The ultimate transformation or change the product will create

**Goals**:
- Grab attention immediately
- Establish your authority
- Present the opportunity for transformation
- Begin raising objections (to address later)

**Content Strategy**:
- Tell origin stories or case studies of transformation
- Paint a picture of what's possible
- Show the gap between where they are and where they could be
- Use "show don't tell" - demonstrate through stories and examples
- End with a call-to-action for engagement (comments, questions)

**Mental Triggers Activated**: Authority, Reciprocity (free value), Community (engagement)

### PLC #2: Transformation

**Core Question**: "What is possible?"
**Focus**: Solid teaching that demonstrates transformation is achievable

**Goals**:
- Deliver genuine teaching and value
- Show that the transformation is real and achievable
- Help prospects see themselves achieving the change
- Continue addressing objections
- Build trust through expertise

**Content Strategy**:
- Teach a specific tip, technique, or concept from your methodology
- Provide quick wins or "aha moments"
- Use before/after examples or case studies
- Recap objections raised in PLC #1 and systematically address them
- Show different paths to transformation
- Ask for feedback and questions

**Mental Triggers Activated**: Authority, Trust, Reciprocity, Social Proof (if using testimonials)

### PLC #3: Ownership Experience

**Core Question**: "How can I achieve this?"
**Focus**: Taking ownership of the future and pivoting to the offer

**Goals**:
- Help prospects take ownership of their future transformation
- Explain the "big view" of how transformation works
- Pivot naturally to your offer (the "soft landing")
- Create anticipation for Open Cart
- Address final objections

**Content Strategy**:
- Show the big picture/framework of your methodology
- Preview what working with your product/program is like
- Use "imagine if..." language to help them visualize success
- Introduce your offer as the vehicle for their transformation
- Announce when the cart opens
- Create urgency around limited availability or bonuses
- Make the pivot feel natural: "You've seen the opportunity, you've seen it's possible, now let me show you the exact path..."

**Mental Triggers Activated**: Anticipation, Scarcity (early introduction), Ownership

### Prelaunch Execution Details:

**Format Options**:
- Video (most common and effective) - full motion or screen capture
- Live broadcasts (creates event energy)
- Email sequences with text-based teaching
- PDF reports or downloads
- Combination approaches

**Timing Guidelines**:
- Lower-priced products ($100-$500): 5-7 day sequences
- Mid-priced products ($500-$2000): 7-10 day sequences
- High-priced products ($2000+): 10+ day sequences
- Release one PLC piece every 2-3 days typically
- Can compress to consecutive days for live launches

**The Launch Conversation**:
- Continuously ask for feedback and questions
- Engage with comments authentically
- Use feedback to refine messaging
- Create a community feeling through shared dialogue
- Make prospects feel heard and valued

## 3. Open Cart Sequence

**Duration**: Typically 4-7 days
**Purpose**: Convert prospects into customers through enrollment

### The Opening:
- Send a simple, short email with a link to the sales page
- Leverage all the anticipation built during Prelaunch
- Expect approximately 25% of total sales on Day 1

### Ongoing Strategy:

**Days 1-2**: Focus on Social Proof
- Share early results: "The response has been incredible..."
- Feature testimonials and early success stories
- Continue answering questions
- Reinforce key benefits

**Days 3-5**: Build Momentum
- Address remaining objections
- Share more case studies
- Highlight bonuses
- Remind of the deadline

**Final 24-48 Hours**: Scarcity and Urgency
- Shift messaging entirely to the deadline
- Send multiple emails on the final day (morning, afternoon, final hours)
- Expect approximately 50% of total sales on the final day
- Use countdown timers
- Final call-to-action emails

### Scarcity Mechanisms (Must be REAL):
1. **Price Increase**: Launch price returns to regular price
2. **Bonus Removal**: Specific high-value bonuses disappear
3. **Offer Closes Completely**: No way to purchase until the next launch

### Advanced Open Cart Tactics:
- PLC #4: Additional bite-sized content focused on guiding to purchase
- FAQ Page or Live Q&A: Address all remaining objections
- Case Study Videos: Show real results from beta testers or past clients
- Student/Celebrity Panels: Social proof and relatability
- Behind-the-scenes content: Build connection and trust
- Email + Social Media coordination: Maximize reach without list fatigue

**Critical Rule**: A definitive close date is absolutely essential. Scarcity drives decision-making.

---

## 4. Post-Launch Sequence

**Duration**: Ongoing
**Purpose**: Strengthen relationships and set up future launches

### For Buyers:
- Over-deliver immediately with unannounced bonuses
- Provide exceptional onboarding
- Create quick wins to reinforce purchase decision
- Build community among customers
- Gather testimonials and case studies
- Reduce buyer's remorse through value delivery

### For Non-Buyers:
- Follow up with value (don't abandon them)
- Survey to understand objections
- Nurture for future launches
- Continue building relationships
- Segment list based on behavior

### Strategic Goals:
- Strengthen brand and reputation
- Generate social proof for the next launch
- Build list for the next launch
- Identify product improvement opportunities
- Create advocates and potential affiliates
