# LaunchMap Builder

Build a complete 9-step business launch strategy (LaunchMap) by guiding the user through
the Fletcher Method framework. The output is a single, growing document that becomes a
comprehensive business blueprint.

## How This Skill Works

Follow the 9 steps below in order. At each step:
1. Explain the objective to the user
2. Ask questions in batches of 3-5 (never dump all questions at once)
3. Collect and organize their answers
4. Update the cumulative LaunchMap document with the new section
5. Get explicit confirmation before proceeding to the next step

If a user asks about a specific step (e.g., "help me with my lead magnet"), orient them
within the overall 9-step process but focus on their requested step. Always reference where
it fits in the bigger picture.

**Key Principle:** Build ONE cumulative LaunchMap artifact/document that grows with each step.
Do not create separate documents for each step.

---

## Key Principles (Apply Throughout)

- **Sequential process** — Complete each step before moving on; earlier steps inform later ones
- **One cumulative document** — Build a single LaunchMap that grows, never separate docs per step
- **Product roadmap is the nucleus** — Everything (content, ads, lead magnets, sales scripts) lives inside the product roadmap
- **Million Dollar Message format everywhere** — Use avatar + currency + metric + timeline - pain for every title, hook, and CTA
- **Context over information** — Every piece of content references where it fits in the overall system
- **Imperfect action beats perfect planning** — Encourage users to take their best shot and refine later
- **Checkpoints matter** — In the enrollment script especially, never skip checkpoints
- **Hot currencies first** — Always lead with the most attractive, bright-shiny elements of the product roadmap for advertising and lead magnets

# STEP 1: PERFECT AVATAR

**Objective:** Create a comprehensive customer profile and target market definition.

The Perfect Avatar consists of three sequential exercises that build a comprehensive
customer profile. This is the foundation for every other step in the LaunchMap.

---

## 1A. Target Market Matchmaker

The Target Market Matchmaker is a rough initial assessment to ensure there's a viable
market worth pursuing. It's designed for quick decision-making, not perfection.

### Questions to Guide the User

**Passion:**
- Who are you most passionate about helping over the long term?
- What defined group of people (avatar/niche/market segment) would you enjoy solving
  one or more major problems for?

**Problems:**
- List a few important problems you can solve for that audience
- Which one stands out as the MOST important problem?
- This is a gut-level answer, not deep market research

**Examples of Problem Statements:**
- "I'm going to help businesses get more sales"
- "Help people lose weight"
- "Help people gain more confidence"
- "Find intimacy or start a relationship"
- "Get a better job"
- "Help local dentists get more patients"

**Profit:**
- What would it be worth for your audience to solve this problem? (Go high — if it's
  only worth $10, it's hard to scale)
- What are people currently paying to solve this problem? (Existing spend = good sign)
- What's your best guess about what you'll charge for your program/product?

**Pathway:**
- Where will you find your target audience?
- Local events requiring travel?
- Online via YouTube, Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter?
- Can you reach them through advertising?

**Key Principle:** "If you have a brand new idea or product, you're either a fool or a genius.
We want you to go down the middle and find a target market that has an existing problem — an
important problem — and solve it more effectively than anybody else."

---

## 1B. Avatar Snapshot

The Avatar Snapshot defines the specific demographic and psychographic profile of the
ideal customer. This informs targeting, messaging, and content strategy.

### Demographics
- Name (give the avatar a real name)
- Gender
- Age range
- Location / geographic area
- Income level
- Marital status
- Occupation / job title
- Education level

### Psychographics
- Daily patterns and routines
- Core beliefs and worldview
- Hobbies and interests
- Motivations and drivers
- Leanings (political, professional, lifestyle)

### Who They Follow
- Experts and thought leaders
- Authors and speakers
- Tools and software they use
- Publications they read
- Associations and groups they belong to
- Podcasts they listen to

### Where They Hang Out
- Social media platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Twitter)
- Online communities and forums
- Offline events, meetups, conferences
- Groups and memberships
- Where competitors are advertising to them

### Avatar Description Statement
Write 1-2 sentences that capture the essence of this person:

**Example:** "Jim is a very entrepreneurial executive who makes $150K per year.
He enjoys networking and self-improvement. He's struggling with scaling his side
business and wants to replace his corporate income within 12 months."

**Template:** "[Name] is a [descriptor] [occupation] who makes [income] per year.
He/she enjoys [interests]. He/she is struggling with [problem] and wants [desired outcome]."

---

## 1C. Avatar Goals

Avatar Goals define the current aspirations, obstacles, and fears of the target customer.
This drives all messaging, content, and sales conversations.

### Current Goals (Top 3)
- What are the top 3 short-term goals your avatar is trying to accomplish?
- These must relate to your main product category
- Example: "Launch their first ad campaign," "Lose 20 pounds," "Close their first
  high-ticket client"

### Why They Want to Achieve Those Goals
- For each goal, ask: "So they can ___?"
- This reveals the long-term aspiration behind the short-term goal
- Example: "Launch an ad campaign SO THEY CAN scale from $10K to $50K/month and
  have weekends off"

**B2C Example (Fitness):**
- Goal: Lose 10 pounds
- So they can: Look and feel better, meet the man of their dreams, live to see
  their children graduate

**B2B Example (Marketing):**
- Goal: Launch an ad campaign
- So they can: Scale from $10K to $50K/month, have weekends off, hire a team,
  achieve financial stability

### Current Obstacles (Top 3 Bricks in the Wall)
- What are the top 3 reasons they can't reach those goals?
- Think of these as bricks in a wall between them and their desired outcome

**B2C Obstacle Example (Fitness):**
1. Don't have time to work out
2. Intimidated by the gym
3. Not motivated

**B2B Obstacle Example (Marketing):**
1. Technical overwhelm — don't understand technology
2. Don't have money to hire an agency
3. Don't have time to learn marketing

### Long-Term Fears (Worst-Case Scenario)
- What happens if they DON'T overcome those obstacles?
- What are the biggest, worst-case scenario fears?

**B2C Fear Example (Fitness):**
- Might get diabetes or serious health risks
- Not be there when children graduate
- Not find a soulmate and get married

**B2B Fear Example (Marketing):**
- Can't scale to $10K-$50K/month
- Going to go out of business
- Can't afford to take kids on vacation
- Stuck working weekends indefinitely

### Research Sources for Goals & Obstacles
- Amazon book 5-star reviews = goals readers achieved
- Amazon book 1-star reviews = problems the book didn't solve
- Competitor ads and blog posts
- Online forums, Facebook groups, Reddit
- Direct conversations with target market
- Quora questions in the topic area

# STEP 2: MILLION DOLLAR MESSAGE

**Objective:** Craft a clear, compelling value proposition using the MDM formula.

The Million Dollar Message (MDM) is the greatest shortcut in copywriting. It provides a
reusable formula for every ad, landing page, content piece, and conversation. The MDM
formula = Single Avatar × Currency + Metric + Timeline − Pain.

---

## 2A. Currency Calculator

The Currency Calculator takes you from a general business category to a specific,
sellable currency.

### Step 1: Name Your Business Category
- What is the primary category of your business?
- Keep it general: "personal training," "online marketing," "financial advising,"
  "life coaching," "web design"
- As if you were at a cocktail party explaining what you do

### Step 2: List All Currencies You Can INCREASE
A currency is a measurable unit of value exchanged for equal or greater value.
"Back in the day, I would trade you a horseshoe for some beef jerky. Currency is
a measurable unit of value that is exchanged or traded with another party for
hopefully equal or greater value."

List everything you can increase for clients:

**Personal Training Example (B2C):**
Energy, muscle mass, confidence, well-being, mental focus, strength, speed,
performance — all of these are currencies a personal trainer can increase.

**Business/Marketing Example (B2B):**
Revenue, leads, conversion rate, profit margin, search rankings, email subscribers,
close rate, appointments, customer lifetime value

**Relationships Example:**
Intimacy, communication, trust, connection, quality time

For each, define:
- **Metric:** How would you measure this? (Can be a scale of 1-5, a percentage,
  a discrete number, or a binary yes/no)
- **Timeline:** In what timeframe can you deliver this result?

**Note on measurement:** "Sometimes people say, 'I can't measure what I do' or
'How do I measure confidence or intimacy?' It doesn't have to be a discrete number
like '10% greater confidence' — that's silly. You know you're COMPLETELY confident.
You know you have ZERO cravings. You can create your own scale. On a scale of 1-5,
complete confidence or not confident at all. You can say zero anxiety. You can say
10% increase in sales."

### Step 3: List All Currencies You Can DECREASE
List everything you can reduce or eliminate:
- Example (fitness): weight, body fat, cravings, anxiety, joint pain
- Example (business): ad spend, employee turnover, time spent on marketing, customer
  acquisition cost
- Example (relationships): conflict, misunderstandings, distance

For each, define metric and timeline.

### Step 4: Pick Your CORE Currency
Your core currency must be:
- **Hot:** Immediately attractive to your audience (use the Currency Temperature Gauge)
- **Specific:** Not vague or generic
- **Easy to sell:** People already want this and are paying for it
- **Measurable:** Can be clearly tracked

**Currency Temperature Gauge:**
- HOT = People actively search for this, competitors sell this, it's bright and shiny
- WARM = People know they need it but aren't actively seeking it
- COLD = Important but not exciting; foundational work

**The "Magic Wand" Test:** Let the customer pick. Imagine saying to your customers:
"If I could wave a magic wand and in 90 days help you do ONE of these things, which
would it be?"

**Personal Training Example — Different avatars pick different currencies:**
- A stay-at-home mom or dad might say: "Weight loss"
- A CrossFit athlete might say: "I want to increase my strength or speed"
- A busy executive might say: "I want to increase my energy, focus, or performance at work"

**The whole point:** This exercise takes you from a CATEGORY (personal training) to a
CURRENCY (weight loss). "A campaign has to be focused around a specific currency, period."

---

## 2B. New Client Filter

Define who you will accept and reject as clients. This is not a fake takeaway — it
protects both you and the client.

### Who to ACCEPT (Ideal Client Attributes)

**Hard Metrics (Specific/Measurable):**
- Current revenue level or business stage
- Years of experience
- Team size
- Current results in the core currency area
- Budget/resources available

**Soft Metrics (Attitude/Behavior):**
- Action taker
- Coachable
- Accountable
- Positive mindset
- Willing to invest time
- Has realistic expectations

**Example (Business Coaching):** "We work with people who are already at $10K or more
a month, are really serious, have the time to invest, are accountable, are coachable,
and are driven to get results in the next 90 days."

### Who to REJECT (Non-Ideal Client Attributes)

**Hard Metrics:**
- Too early stage (can't be helped yet)
- Insufficient resources
- Wrong industry/fit
- Already too advanced

**Soft Metrics:**
- Procrastinator
- Negative attitude
- Not willing to do the work
- Blames others
- Unrealistic expectations
- Not committed to the timeline

**Example (Business Coaching):** "We don't work with people who outsource everything
to someone else, are still struggling in their business, aren't serious, or lack
accountability."

**Key insight:** "You can take someone who has maybe $10K a month in revenue that's
ready to scale up and has the resources, but they're not an action taker, they're a
procrastinator, they're negative, they don't show up, they don't have the time."
Both hard AND soft metrics matter.

---

## 2C. Million Dollar Message Formula

### The Formula
**MDM = Single Avatar × (Currency + Metric + Timeline) − Pain**

### Components

**Single Avatar:** Who specifically are you targeting?
- Use the avatar name/descriptor from Step 1
- Be specific: "established business coaches," "busy executives in their 40s,"
  "local dentists"

**Currency:** What primary result will they achieve?
- From the Currency Calculator — your core currency

**Metric:** How is it measured?
- Specific number, percentage, scale, or clear outcome
- "Scale to $50K/month," "Lose 20 pounds," "Get 200 leads per day"

**Timeline:** How long will it take?
- "In 90 days or less," "In 6 weeks," "In 30 days"

**Pain (Removed):** What frustrations/obstacles are eliminated?
- Pull from Avatar Goals obstacles
- "Without tech overwhelm," "Without setting foot in a gym,"
  "Without cheesy pressure-selling tactics"

### MDM Examples
- "I help established business coaches scale their sales to $50K or more per month
  in 90 days or less without frustration or tech overwhelm."
- "I help passionate executives look and feel in the best shape of their life in
  90 days or less without taking pharmaceutical drugs or setting foot into an
  intimidating gym full of meatheads."
- "I help local dentists get a steady flow of 20+ new patients per month in 60 days
  without spending a fortune on advertising."

### Where to Use the MDM
- Your overall business/brand message
- Each step of your product roadmap (each step gets its own MDM)
- Every lead magnet title
- Every ad headline
- Every authority amplifier video opening
- Every sales conversation
- Every piece of content

# STEP 3: PERFECT OFFER

**Objective:** Structure the offer using the Profit Pyramid, Product Roadmap, and Delivery Checklist.

---

## 3A. Profit Pyramid

Segment the target market into a ladder or pyramid so clients know where they are and
how you can help them get to the next level.

### Phase 1 (Bottom) — Can't Be Helped Yet
- **Segment Title:** [e.g., "Struggling," "Beginner," "Not Ready"]
- **Currency Metric Range:** Where are they with your core currency?
- **Symptoms/Struggles:** What are they experiencing at this level?
- **Keys to Success:** What would they need to do to move up?
- **Note:** These people usually aren't ready for your services

### Phase 2 — Ready for Help (PRIMARY TARGET)
- **Segment Title:** [e.g., "Surviving," "Intermediate," "Emerging"]
- **Currency Metric Range:** [e.g., $5K-$15K/month, lost weight but plateaued]
- **Symptoms/Struggles:** What specific challenges do they face?
- **Keys to Success:** What would move them to the next phase?

### Phase 3 — Active Growth (PRIMARY TARGET)
- **Segment Title:** [e.g., "Thriving," "Advanced," "Growing"]
- **Currency Metric Range:** [e.g., $15K-$50K/month, regular gym-goer seeking optimization]
- **Symptoms/Struggles:** What's holding them back at this level?
- **Keys to Success:** What gets them to the top?

### Phase 4 (Top) — Too Advanced
- **Segment Title:** [e.g., "Driving," "Expert," "Mastered"]
- **Currency Metric Range:** [e.g., $100K+/month, elite athlete]
- **Note:** These people usually don't need your help

**Fletcher Method Example (Revenue & Leads):**
- Phase 1 — **Struggling:** $0-$3K/month. Still have a day job. No marketing system.
- Phase 2 — **Surviving:** $3K-$15K/month. Have clients but inconsistent. Need a system.
- Phase 3 — **Thriving:** $15K-$50K/month. Getting results but can't scale. Need optimization.
- Phase 4 — **Driving:** $50K+/month. Largely self-sufficient. Don't need most help.

**Your job is taking Phase 2 and Phase 3 clients to the next level.**

---

## 3B. Product Roadmap

The Product Roadmap is the NUCLEUS of the entire Fletcher Method. Everything else —
content, ads, lead magnets, sales scripts, webinars — lives inside this roadmap.

### Product Details

**Product Title:**
- Give it a unique name
- Include the avatar AND core currency when possible

**Product Naming Examples:**
- Instead of "The Rapid Weight Loss Roadmap" → "The Busy Executive's Fat-Burning Roadmap"
- "The Hotel 0% Vacancy Rate Roadmap"
- "The Local Dentist's Sold-Out Patients Blueprint"
- "The Online Launch Map"

**Product Million Dollar Message (Tagline):**
- Use the MDM formula for the product tagline
- Example: "The Local Business Domination Roadmap: How Every Local Business Can Get a
  Steady Flow of New Customers Every Month Without Paid Advertising"

**Delivery Model:**
- One-on-one coaching/consulting
- Group coaching/mastermind
- Online course (self-paced)
- Done-for-you services
- Hybrid (combination)
- Will you do it WITH them, let them do it THEMSELVES, or do it FOR them?

**Product Pricing:**
- What will you charge?
- Payment structure (one-time, payment plan, recurring)

### Product Roadmap Structure

Organize the solution into 3 STAGES, each containing STEPS (aim for ~3 steps per stage,
totaling ~9 steps). Think of this as the ultimate instruction manual.

**Real-World Member Examples:**
- How to become a professional cheerleader
- Passing the bar exam
- Launching a seven-figure law practice
- Increasing intimacy and romance
- Getting a steady flow of dental patients

**How to brainstorm:**
1. Use Post-it notes, mind mapping, or whiteboard
2. Ask: "What are ALL the things someone must accomplish to go from Point A to Point B,
   even if they don't work with me?"
3. Group them into natural categories
4. Most roadmaps follow this pattern:
   - Foundation phase (mindset, planning, analysis)
   - Planning/Strategy phase (resources, strategy creation)
   - Execution phase (launch, refine, scale)

**For each STAGE:**
- Stage title
- Stage description
- What major milestone does completing this stage represent?

**For each STEP within a stage:**
- Unique title
- Core currency or problem this step solves
- Brief description
- What result does the client achieve by completing this step?

### Fletcher Method Product Roadmap Example
- **Stage A: Foundation**
  - Step 1: Perfect Avatar (target market definition)
  - Step 2: Million Dollar Message (messaging & positioning)
  - Step 3: Perfect Offer (product roadmap & pricing)
- **Stage B: Funnel**
  - Step 4: Ultimate Lead Magnet (lead generation)
  - Step 5: Authority Amplifier (content & authority building)
  - Step 6: Conversion Event / 10X Enrollment Script (sales)
- **Stage C: Traffic**
  - Step 7: Content Roadmap (content strategy)
  - Step 8: Traffic On Demand (advertising)
  - Step 9: Retargeting Roadmap (follow-up & scaling)

**Key insight:** "You're giving your audience a clear path, possibly for the first time.
It's like saying, 'Of course you're struggling to get results — you're not following a
game plan.' Doctors, lawyers, chefs — any profession follows a structured path. You're
giving your audience that same clear path."

**Key Principle:** "This is NOT about you, your products, or a list of your services.
Think of it as a book — if you were to write the ultimate instruction manual for achieving
results, what would it include?"

3. Define your core currency
4. Add a metric and timeline

# STEP 4: ULTIMATE LEAD MAGNET

**Objective:** Design lead magnets that align with the product roadmap.

Your product roadmap solves one BIG problem through 9 steps. Each step solves a specific
sub-problem, has its own currency, has its own Million Dollar Message, can be turned into
a lead magnet, can be advertised independently, and feeds back into the overall system.
This gives you 10 lead magnet opportunities: 1 for the whole system + 1 for each step.

---

## 4A. Rate Product Roadmap Steps (Hot/Warm/Cold)

Review each step of your product roadmap and rate its attractiveness to cold traffic:

**HOT** = Bright, shiny, immediately attractive. Usually execution-focused steps.
Things people actively want: templates, scripts, cheat sheets, shortcuts.

**WARM** = People know they need it but aren't actively searching for it.

**COLD** = Foundational, necessary but not sexy. Cold traffic doesn't understand why
they need this yet.

**Key insight:** "People want the bright, shiny things. It's usually towards the top
of your product roadmap, not the bottom."

**Fletcher Method Hot/Warm/Cold Rating Example:**
- Avatar checklist → COLD (people don't think they need this)
- Million Dollar Message framework → WARM (interesting but not urgent)
- Product Roadmap builder → COLD-WARM (cold traffic doesn't understand how different it is)
- Ultimate Lead Magnet checklist → HOT ("How to create a high-converting lead magnet")
- Sales video script → HOT
- 10X Enrollment Script (sales script) → HOT ("How to close high-ticket deals")
- Facebook Ads template → HOT (people actively want this)

---

## 4B. Core Ultimate Lead Magnet

The Core ULM covers your ENTIRE product roadmap — it's the lead magnet for your whole
system/methodology.

### Components

**Title:** Choose a unique, compelling title that includes the core currency or result.
Examples: "The Online Launch Map," "The Fat-Burning Blueprint," "The Client Attraction Playbook"

**Million Dollar Message:** The MDM for your entire product/program (same as product MDM from Step 3).

**Metaphor or Hook:** A creative analogy that makes it memorable and tangible.

**Creative/Format:**
- Checklist (most common and effective)
- Cheat sheet
- Template
- Blueprint/roadmap diagram
- Quick-start guide
- Assessment/scorecard

**Key principle:** Keep it simple. A one-page checklist that solves one problem works
better than a 50-page ebook that overwhelms.

"When it comes to lead magnets, people think creating something super complex that
takes forever works. What you want to do is just give them one checklist that solves
one problem. You want to pick the hottest step and create a lead magnet around that."

"If this doesn't work, you're probably not going to sell anything to anybody." — Alex Becker

---

## 4C. Step-Specific Lead Magnets

Start with the 2-3 HOTTEST steps from your product roadmap. For each:

**Title:** Unique and compelling, references the specific problem this step solves.

**Million Dollar Message:** Specific to THIS step, not the whole product.

**Metaphor or Hook:** Relate it to their life in a vivid way.

**Creative/Format:** Use the delivery checklist from Step 3 as the basis. Every step
already has a checklist — just turn it into a downloadable PDF. Image of the actual
checklist is the ad creative (not stock photos, not photos of you).

**Complete Example — Fletcher Method Sales Script Lead Magnet:**
- **Title:** "The 10X Enrollment Script"
- **Million Dollar Message:** "How to close 20-50% of the prospects you talk to with
  zero pressure selling or experience required."
- **Metaphor/Hook:** "Like having a line of qualified prospects waiting to interview
  to work with you with zero pressure selling." Or: "A starving crowd of people wanting
  to talk to you five days a week."
- **Format:** One-page sales script template / checklist

**Complete Example — Email Sequence Lead Magnet:**
- **Title:** "The Automated Follow-Up Machine"
- **Million Dollar Message:** "How to nurture leads into buyers on autopilot"
- **Metaphor/Hook:** "Like having an army of robots selling on your behalf while
  you sleep" — much more powerful than saying "an automated email sequence"
- **Format:** Email template swipe file

---

### The Funnel Connection

Each lead magnet creates a mini funnel:
1. **Ad** — Features image of the checklist/cheat sheet with MDM headline
2. **Landing Page** — Same image, same headline, name + email capture
3. **Authority Amplifier Video** — Walk them through this one step
4. **Conversion Event** — Book a call, buy a product, or attend a webinar

All mini funnels feed into the same conversion event.

# STEP 5: AUTHORITY AMPLIFIER

**Objective:** Create the content delivery framework for every video, presentation, and sales message.

The Authority Amplifier is the format for EVERY communication: marketing, advertising,
social media, sales videos, webinars, and content. It consists of three phases: Framing,
Content, and Action.

There are two types:
1. **Core AA** — Covers your entire product roadmap (flagship content)
2. **Step AAs** — One for each step of your product roadmap (mini campaigns)

---

## 5A. Framing Phase

The Framing Phase hooks the viewer and establishes credibility in the first few seconds.

### Title
- Use the Million Dollar Message format
- Examples: "The 10X Enrollment Script," "The Seven-Day Cleansing Cheat Sheet"

### Promise
Start with: "In this quick video, I'm going to show you..."

**Example (Lead Magnet step):**
"In this video, I'll show you the one type of lead magnet we use to generate 200-300
leads per day for $2 each — without any split testing or treating Facebook like a day
trader. This lead magnet has worked for years without needing constant updates, so pay
attention."

**Example (Sales Script step):**
"In this quick video, I'm going to show you the simple one-page sales script we've used
to close hundreds of high-ticket deals without any pressure-selling tactics or cheesy BS,
even with little or no sales experience."

### Proof (3 Levels)

**Level 1 — Personal Results:**
"This is the exact system/process I use to [get specific result]."
Example: "I've used this to grow several seven-figure businesses."

**Level 2 — Client Results:**
"Our X number of customers/clients have used this to [get specific results]."
Example: "Our clients use this same script every day to close high-ticket deals."

**Level 3 — Industry Validation:**
"Virtually every successful [professional] in [industry] uses some version of this."
Example: "Virtually every successful high-ticket salesperson on the planet uses these elements."

**Dentist Example for Borrowed Authority:** "If you're helping dentists scale their
practices, you can point out that no successful dental practice is growing beyond a
certain level without implementing this strategy. You're curating and condensing what
already works in the real world."

**If you're new and don't have testimonials:** Lead with industry validation. Share the
methodology/research behind your approach. Use your own results, even if small.

---

## 5B. Content Phase

### Problems (State 3)
"The problem is there are [3] reasons why people struggle with [topic]:"
1. Problem/reason #1
2. Problem/reason #2
3. Problem/reason #3

Pull from market research, online forums, competitor content, Amazon book reviews,
and direct conversations.

### Steps (Show the Path)
"The good news is we've broken this down into [X] simple steps:"

**Critical:** Show WHAT to do, not all of the HOW. The how is what they buy.
For the Core AA: Walk through all 9 steps at a high level.
For Step AAs: Walk through the sub-steps from your delivery checklist.

---

## 5C. Action Phase

### Context
- For Step AAs: "What we just covered is Step [X] of a [9]-step system that helps
  [avatar] [achieve currency + metric] in [timeline] without [pain]."
- For the Core AA: Context is already built in.

### Action (Single Conversion Event)
Drive to ONE action: Book a call, buy a product, or attend a webinar.

Explain WHY: "Because we're going to sit down and map out your 90-day strategy..."

# STEP 6: 10X ENROLLMENT SCRIPT

**Objective:** Create a diagnostic sales conversation framework with checkpoints.

This is a clinical, diagnostic approach: identify the problem, give a prescription, and
invite them to work with you. "If we do everything upfront and conduct the needs analysis
properly, there is no need for closing tactics, cheesy scarcity, or takeaways at the end."

---

## 6A. FRAME

### Goals
1. "We're going to identify the exact steps you should be taking to get [results/currency]."
2. "We're going to determine if and how we should be working together."
- "Does that make sense?"

### Control
- "This call will last exactly [X] minutes."
- "I'm going to move quickly to ensure we leave with 100% clarity."

### ✅ CHECKPOINT — Frame
1. "Are you okay with the goals and format of this call?" → YES
2. "Are you ready to solve this problem now if we agree this is a good fit?" → YES
3. "Are you empowered to make a decision today?" → YES

---

## 6B. DISCOVER

### Business Goals (Two Key Numbers)
1. "Where are you RIGHT NOW when it comes to [core currency]?"
2. "Where do you want to be in [timeline that matches your product]?"
3. "Why does that matter? Why not just stay where you are?"

### Product Gaps (The Game-Changer)
Audit the prospect against your product roadmap steps.

**Fletcher Method Product Gap Audit Example:**
"Let's find out your exact status on the 9 essential elements of a successful program
to help you get to $40K in 90 days."

1. "Who's your target customer?" → Well-defined niche? Big enough?
2. "What is your message? How do you articulate what you do?" → Strong positioning?
3. "What's your product and pricing?" → Properly structured?
4. "What lead magnets are you using?" → Generating leads?
5. "What does your authority content look like?" → Building trust?
6. "How are you selling — what's your conversion process?" → Effective sales system?
7. "What content are you creating and where?" → Consistent strategy?
8. "How are you driving traffic?" → Paid and organic?
9. "What follow-up/retargeting do you have?" → Maximizing conversions?

"Small hinges swing big doors. What they see as insurmountable obstacles — these huge
walls — might actually be minor adjustments. A client might simply need to tweak their
pricing or switch from using a complicated webinar to a simple authority amplifier video."

### ✅ CHECKPOINT — Discover
1. "So let me get this clear — you're at [current], trying to get to [desired], correct?"
2. "How urgent is solving this problem for you?"

---

## 6C. PROBLEMS

### Struggles
- "What are your biggest challenges when it comes to [core currency]?"
- "What methods have you tried?" (Courses, coaches, events, DIY)
- "How much time and money have you spent?"
- "How long have you been trying to solve this?"

### Cost of Not Solving (The Problem Behind the Problem)
- "Is this affecting anything else in your life? Tell me about that."

**Example Dialogue — The Soccer Games:**
"We know you want to get to $50K, and you've been stuck here for five years, taking
all these courses and spending $20K on coaching. But how is this affecting you? Is it
affecting your personal life?"

"Well, I'm at the office every weekend."

"Why is it important to you to have weekends off? If in 90 days you had weekends off,
what would you do?"

"I'd actually attend my kid's soccer games. That means the world to me — I want to be
a good father, and I feel like I'm failing."

THAT is meaningful. Now we understand the real cost of staying stuck.

### ✅ CHECKPOINT — Problems (Critical)
1. "How ready are you to solve this RIGHT NOW?"
2. "If you were completely confident you could solve this problem, would you do it right now?"
3. "What would it be worth to get [desired result] in [timeline]?"

**Example — Value Confirmation:**
"You've been stuck at $10K, and you want to get to $40K. If you were 100% confident
you could get there in 90 days, what would it be worth to you?"

Good response: "I've already paid $100K for coaches, and I'd pay another $50K easily
if I knew it would work." → Great, proceed.

Bad response: "$100." → You MUST push back.

**Example — The $500 Pushback Dialogue:**
"Wait a minute. If it's only worth $500 to you, tell me why. I just want to be clear.
Because you're telling me you've been struggling with this problem for three years,
you're not seeing your children, and you've said you would pay anything to be a better
father. You also told me that if you were confident in the process, you would be making
another $30K per month at $50K. So tell me why you're saying $500."

**Do NOT proceed unless** the value they state is equal to or greater than what you will charge.

---

## 6D. PRESCRIPTION

### Permission
"With your permission, I'd like to spend a few moments outlining a strategy I believe
will help you reach your goals. Does that sound okay?"

### Prescription (3 Core Actions)
Pull the 3 most powerful insights from the product gap audit.

### ✅ CHECKPOINT — Prescription
1. "Repeat back to me — what do you think the three keys to victory would be?"
2. "On a scale of 1-5, how confident are you?" **Must be a 5 to proceed.**

---

## 6E. APPLICATION

### Transformation
"We have a unique program designed to help [avatar] achieve [currency] in [timeline].
It's called [product name]. It's very different from what you've been doing because [differentiators]."

**Critical:** Do NOT list features, modules, or coaching calls. Describe the TRANSFORMATION.

**The Two Gyms Analogy:**

GYM #1 (BAD — Feature Dumping):
"Hey, I'm a personal trainer. We have these eight machines that get you totally exhausted,
activating all your muscles through advanced biometric training. Then we measure your body
fat every morning at 5 a.m. We give you a strict macro-based diet program and make sure
you're accountable. We have this tracking application, and you're going to measure your meals."

Your reaction: "Whoa, I'm overwhelmed. Get me out of here."

GYM #2 (GOOD — Transformation Focus):
"Hey, we help busy executives like you in their forties and fifties look and feel in the
best shape of their lives without spending hours in the gym, taking crazy diet pills, or
following extreme meal plans — without giving up the food they love. That's what we do
each and every day. And I'd like to find out if this is a good fit for you."

Your reaction: "Cool. Sign me up."

### Qualification
"For most folks we talk to, this is NOT a fit." State accept/reject criteria.
"Tell me which of those sound most like you, and why."

### ✅ CHECKPOINT — Application
1. "How do you think this is different from what you've tried before?"
2. "Why do you think you might be a good fit?"

---

## 6F. INVITATION

### The Invitation
"Based on what we've covered today — we know where you are, where you want to go, and
we're both very confident this would be a great opportunity. I'd like to invite you to
the program. If we can agree on logistics, are you ready to get started?"

### Price Delivery
- "The investment is [price]."
- **SILENCE.** Do not talk yourself out of the sale.

### Objection Handling
**Key principle:** "Yeses make me money. No's save me money. Maybes cost me money."

**Example — "I've tried other courses":**
"We talked about why this is different, but you tell me — are you looking to do whatever
it takes to get to $50K, or are you concerned with the number of modules?"

**Example — "Let me talk to my partner":**
"We already agreed you're empowered to make a decision. If you want to bring your partner
in, when should we do that together?"

**The real issue:** "People don't take action because they don't believe in themselves.
They have a fear of success, a fear of failure, anxiety, imposter syndrome. Give them
the opportunity to see that bigger version of themselves."

### Expectations / Wins Matrix

**Fletcher Method Wins Matrix Example:**
- **First hour:** "You'll get your login and access and instantly have all the tools."
- **First week:** "You'll have your first group session and will have already started."
- **First month:** "You'll get clear on your message and have your first ad running."

Don't end with the transaction — start serving immediately.

# STEP 9: RETARGETING ROADMAP

**Objective:** Build a follow-up system using TOF, MOF, and BOF content strategies.

---

## 9A. Sales Funnel Steps & Metrics Multiplier

### List Your Funnel Steps
Audience building → Lead capture → Authority Amplifier → Conversion → Sale

### Metrics Multiplier (Reverse-Engineer Your Goal)
1. Customer value: $[X]
2. Strategy session value: Customer Value ÷ 5 (at 20% close rate) = $[X]
3. Lead value: Session Value ÷ 20 (at 5% booking rate) = $[X]
4. Revenue goal ÷ customer value = customers needed → × 5 = sessions → × 20 = leads

**Example:** Goal $20K/month. At $5K/customer = 4 customers. Need ~25 sessions. Need ~300 leads.

---

## 9B. Top-of-Funnel (TOF) Content

Build an engaged audience BEFORE capturing leads.

**Cost comparison:**
- Video views: $0.03-$0.05 each
- Cold lead capture: $3-$10+ each
- Retargeted lead capture: $1-$3 each (much cheaper)

Select 3-4 hottest content pieces. Create short value-first videos.

**Example TOF Video:**
"Here's one strategy we use to cut lead costs in half. It's called the Ultimate Lead
Magnet. Let me walk you through it." End with: "If you want to learn how the whole
system works, go through this full training here."

---

## 9C. Middle-of-Funnel (MOF) Content

### The Bow Tie
"Think of it like a bow tie — it's just your core content, your flagship piece of content,
where you're walking people through your entire product roadmap."

Core MOF Funnel: Landing page → Authority Amplifier video → Scheduling/purchase page.
Target: 5% of warm audience books calls in 30 days.

**MOF Conversion Math Example:**
20,000 audience → 1,000 leads (5%) → 50 sessions (5%) → 10 customers (20%) → $50,000

---

## 9D. Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF) Content

Convert fence-sitters over 15-30 days:
1. **FAQs** — Answer top objections
2. **Testimonials** — Client success stories
3. **Product Tour** — Show what's inside
4. **Engagement Ads** — "What's your #1 question about [topic]?"
5. **Incentives** — Free trial, bonus, guarantee

**Key BOF Insight:** "Don't underestimate the fear and apprehension people have about
seeing what's on the other side. If you have a high-quality product, be proud — say,
'Here's a couple of sample training videos.' People want to see what it's really like."
