# PLF ESSENTIAL METRICS AND BENCHMARKS

ESSENTIAL METRICS AND BENCHMARKS ### Email Metrics - **Open Rates**: 20-40%+ for engaged lists - **Click Rates**: 3-10%+ for launch emails - **List Growth**: Aim for 20-50% between launches ### Content Metrics - **Video Completion Rate**: 50%+ for PLC videos - **Engagement Rate**: Comments, shares, questions on each piece - **Survey Response Rate**: 20-40%+ for Pre-Pre surveys ### Sales Metrics - **Conversion Rate**: - Low-ticket ($100-$500): 3-10% - Mid-ticket ($500-$2000): 1-5% - High-ticket ($2000+): 0.5-3% - **Revenue Per Subscriber**: $1-$10+ depending on price point - **Day 1 Sales**: ~25% of total - **Final Day Sales**: ~50% of total ### Post-Launch Metrics - **Customer Satisfaction**: Survey immediately post-launch - **Refund Rate**: Should be under 5% - **Testimonial Rate**: Aim for 10%+ of buyers - **Repeat Buyer Rate**: Track for future launches --- ## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID 1. **Skipping the Prelaunch**: Going straight to the pitch without delivering value 2. **Weak or Artificial Scarcity**: Scarcity that isn't real or meaningful 3. **Not Setting a Deadline**: Allowing cart to stay open indefinitely 4. **Ignoring Objections**: Failing to surface and address concerns 5. **Poor List Building**: Not growing list between launches 6. **Skipping Seed Launch**: Trying to do JV launch without proof 7. **Over-Promising**: Making claims you can't support 8. **Under-Delivering in PLC**: Holding back value or teaching too little 9. **Inconsistent Messaging**: Not maintaining story arc across sequence 10. **Not Following Up Post-Launch**: Abandoning new customers or non-buyers

# The Circle of Awesome™ - Continuous Growth Cycle

The Business Launch Formula is a cyclical approach to building a sustainable, growing business:

**The Cycle**:
1. **Seed Launch**: Create a product, validate the market, and build an initial list.
2. **Internal Launch**: Test and optimize, grow the list, and improve the offer.
3. **JV Launch**: Scale dramatically, establish authority, and achieve massive list growth.
4. **New Product Idea**: Begin the cycle again with a new Seed Launch.

**Continuous Improvement Loop**:
*   Each launch generates cash flow.
*   Each launch builds your list.
*   Each launch strengthens your market position.
*   Each launch generates new product ideas.
*   Each launch builds momentum.

**Long-Term Strategy**:
*   Avoid doing just one launch.
*   Plan multiple launches per year.
*   Evolve offers based on market feedback.
*   Build towards recurring revenue (e.g., membership sites, continuity).
*   Create a launch calendar aligned with market needs.

## The Sideways Sales Letter

**Concept**: Instead of cramming everything into a long-form sales page, spread value delivery over multiple days or pieces of content.

**Why It Works**:
*   Cuts through communication fog with sequenced messages.
*   Builds relationships over time.
*   Transforms monologue into dialogue.
*   Allows for real-time objection handling.
*   Creates anticipation and event energy.
*   Leverages multiple mental triggers simultaneously.

**Execution**:
*   Each PLC piece is like a chapter in a story.
*   The story arc moves from opportunity → transformation → ownership.
*   Sales messaging is woven throughout, not concentrated at the end.
*   Engagement at each step refines the next steps.
*   The "pitch" becomes a natural conclusion of the value journey.

## Advanced Launch Strategies

### Live Launches

**Strategy**: Deliver PLC and/or Open Cart content via live broadcasts.

**Platforms**: Facebook Live, YouTube Live, Instagram Live, Zoom webinars.

**Benefits**:
*   Creates ephemeral event energy.
*   Higher engagement per viewer.
*   Real-time Q&A and objection handling.
*   Replay scarcity adds urgency.
*   More authentic and personal.

**Execution Tips**:
*   Promote live times heavily.
*   Offer replay but emphasize live attendance.
*   Make it interactive (Q&A, polls, comments).
*   Repurpose recordings afterward.
*   Stack live events throughout the launch.

### Evergreen Launches

**Strategy**: Automated launch sequence triggered when someone joins the list.

**Structure**:
*   New subscriber enters an automated sequence.
*   Receives PLC over the next 7-10 days.
*   Cart opens on day X for 4-7 days just for them.
*   Cart closes with a real deadline.
*   Cycles continuously for new subscribers.

**Requirements**:
*   Sophisticated email automation.
*   Evergreen sales page (no specific dates).
*   Automated deadline enforcement.
*   Proven offer that converts consistently.

**Benefits**:
*   Generate revenue continuously.
*   Scale without manual launches.
*   Perfect for consistent lead flow.
*   Maximize the value of each subscriber.

### Relaunch Strategy

**When to Relaunch**:
*   Every 3-6 months typically.
*   When you have significant new list growth.
*   When you've improved the offer.
*   Seasonal or timely opportunities.

**Relaunch Variations**:
*   Full sequence to the entire list.
*   Shortened sequence to past buyers (upsell/cross-sell).
*   Focus on new features or bonuses.
Different market positioning or angle.
