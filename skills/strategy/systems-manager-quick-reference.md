## WHEN TO USE EACH TOOL
### Use SCORECARD when:
✓ You need to track performance ✓ Someone needs to own a number ✓ You want public accountability ✓ You need to spot trends
### Use WEEKLY SYNC when:
✓ Aligning the team on priorities ✓ Discussing red/yellow metrics ✓ Making group decisions ✓ Getting commitment to action
### Use 1-ON-1s when:
✓ Coaching someone on performance ✓ Addressing specific behavioral issues ✓ Teaching principles, not tasks ✓ Building trust privately
### Use PROCESS DOCS when:
✓ Work needs to be repeatable ✓ Multiple people do the same task ✓ Training new team members ✓ Reducing errors and confusion

## EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
### WHEN SCORECARD ISN'T UPDATED
1. Send reminder 24 hours before sync
2. Call directly if not updated by deadline
3. Skip their report in meeting
4. Address in private 1-on-1 afterward
### WHEN MEETING GOES OFF TRACK
1. Interrupt politely: "Let's stay focused on [topic]"
2. Use parking lot for off-topic items
3. Timebox discussions (8 min max)
4. End meeting at 60 min regardless
### WHEN METRIC STAYS RED 3+ WEEKS
1. Diagnose: Skill or will problem?
2. Have accountability conversation
3. Create recovery plan with milestones
4. If no improvement in 2 more weeks → role fit issue
### WHEN CEO STARTS MICROMANAGING
1. Show them Outcome-Measure-Coach framework
2. Remind: "We teach people how to treat us"
3. Redirect to weekly coaching instead of daily fixes
4. Get commitment to let team solve problems
* * *
## QUICK DECISION MATRIX
### SHOULD I SOLVE THIS OR COACH THEM?
**SOLVE IT yourself if:**
*   It's a systems/tools problem outside their control
*   It requires budget approval
*   It's a one-time crisis needing immediate action
*   They lack authority to fix it
*   Business can't afford the learning time
**COACH THEM if:**
*   It's a skill they need to develop
*   They have authority to solve it
*   It's a learning opportunity
*   They'll face this again
*   Solution requires their expertise

## ONE-SENTENCE SCRIPTS
### For meetings running long:
_"We're at time. What's the decision and who owns it?"_
### For people rambling during reporting:
_"Let's capture that for discussion. What's your next metric?"_
### For off-topic tangents:
_"That's important—let's park it for later. Back to [topic]."_
### For problem-solving during reporting:
_"Great discussion topic. I'm writing it down for later."_
### For unprepared team members:
_"We'll skip your section and discuss in our 1-on-1."_
### For getting commitment:
_"What's your specific commitment going forward?"_
### For accountability:
_"Your name is on this number. How will you move it?"_
### For coaching:
_"What do you think you should do differently?"_

## RED FLAGS CHEAT SHEET
### DATA PROBLEM
**Symptoms:** Numbers seem off, inconsistent, or questionable **Fix:** Verify data source, update tracking method, document process
### SKILL PROBLEM
**Symptoms:** They don't know HOW to do it **Fix:** Provide training, coaching, resources, or mentor
### WILL PROBLEM
**Symptoms:** They know how but aren't doing it **Fix:** Accountability conversation, reset expectations, check commitment
### SYSTEMS PROBLEM
**Symptoms:** Process/tools blocking them **Fix:** Remove blocker, create workaround, improve system
### PATTERN PROBLEM
**Symptoms:** Metric red for 3+ weeks **Fix:** Serious intervention, possible role change

## MEETING QUALITY QUICK CHECK
**Score each YES = 1 point | NO = 0 points**
□ Meeting started on time □ Meeting ended on time (60 min max) □ All metrics were updated before meeting □ Everyone reported their numbers □ We discussed red/yellow flags □ Clear actions were assigned with owners □ Specific deadlines were set □ We got verbal confirmations on commitments □ Meeting was rated 8+ out of 10 □ Notes sent within 2 hours
**Score:**
*   9-10: Excellent
*   7-8: Good, minor tweaks needed
*   5-6: Needs improvement
*   Below 5: Major issues to fix
