# PLF THE THREE TYPES OF LAUNCHES

# The Three Types of Launches

### 1\. Seed Launch® - Starting From Scratch

**When to Use**:
*   You have a small or no email list (30-300 people).
*   You have an idea but no product yet.
*   You want to test market interest before creating.
*   You're starting a new business or product line.

**Strategy**:
1. Build a small micro-list (even 30 people is enough).
2. Offer to teach them live (webinars, teleclasses, group coaching).
3. They pay upfront for the experience.
4. Deliver content live and record it.
5. Use recordings to create the final product.

**Benefits**:
*   Generate revenue while creating the product.
*   Validate market interest before investing time in creation.
*   Get real-time feedback to improve the product.
*   Learn to teach material effectively.
*   Build an initial customer base and gather testimonials.
*   Create a product from recordings and transcripts.

**Execution**:
*   Simplified PLF sequence (may skip formal Pre-Pre).
*   Focus on transformation promise.
*   Price appropriately for beta/founding members.
*   Emphasize the collaborative creation process.
*   Over-deliver on value.

**Example Success Path**:
Starting micro-list → Seed Launch ($5,000-$25,000) → Build list from buyers → Internal Launch ($50,000-$100,000) → Expand list further → JV Launch ($500,000+).

### 2\. Internal Launch - Testing and Optimizing

**When to Use**:
*   You have an existing email list.
*   You want to test the offer before involving partners.
*   You're refining your launch sequence.
*   You're validating product-market fit.

**Strategy**:
*   Use the full PLF sequence (Pre-Pre through Post-Launch).
*   Launch only to your owned list.
*   No affiliate or JV partner involvement.
*   Focus on conversion optimization.
*   Gather detailed metrics and feedback.

**Goals**:
*   Prove the offer converts well.
*   Refine messaging and sequences.
*   Identify and address all objections.
*   Create case studies for future launches.
*   Protect JV relationships by ensuring you have a "winner."
*   Build confidence in the offer.

**Key Metrics to Track**:
*   Open rates for each email.
*   Click-through rates.
*   PLC video view rates and completion rates.
*   Sales conversion percentage.
*   Revenue per subscriber.
*   Objections that surfaced.
*   Customer feedback.

**Before Moving to JV Launch**:
*   Conversion rate should be strong (1-5%+ depending on price point).
*   Offer should be fully validated.
*   All major objections should be addressed.
*   Customer satisfaction should be high.
*   Systems should be proven to handle scale.
* * *

### 3\. JV Launch (Joint Venture/Affiliate Launch) - Exponential Growth

**When to Use**:
*   You have a proven offer (tested via Internal Launch).
*   You want rapid list growth and market positioning.
*   You're ready to scale significantly.
*   You have the infrastructure to handle volume.

**Strategy**:
*   Recruit Joint Venture partners and affiliates.
*   They promote your launch to their lists.
*   You pay commission on sales (typically 50%).
*   Leverage their authority and audiences.
*   Coordinate timing across all partners.

**Partner Recruitment**:
*   Start building relationships early (months in advance).
*   Look for partners whose audiences match your ideal customer.
*   Offer a compelling commission structure.
*   Provide high-quality promotional materials.
*   Make it easy for them to promote.

**Critical Success Factor - EPC (Earnings Per Click)**:
*   Partners care about how much they earn per click they send.
*   High EPC attracts top partners.
*   Proven offer + great launch sequence = high EPC.
*   Aim for $1-$3+ EPC depending on price point.

**Partner Support**:
*   Provide pre-written emails (they'll customize).
*   Create affiliate training webinars.
*   Offer real-time stats and leaderboards.
*   Be responsive to partner questions.
*   Over-communicate throughout the launch.

**Execution Differences**:
*   Coordinate timing across multiple lists.
*   May compress the timeline slightly.
*   Heavier focus on social proof early.
*   Partner-specific bonuses or incentives.
*   More sophisticated tracking and attribution.

**Potential Results**:
*   Massive list growth (10x-100x in one launch).
*   Instant expert positioning in the market.
*   Six to seven-figure revenue in a single launch.
*   Relationships with top influencers.
*   Platform for future collaborations.

**After JV Launch**:
*   Thank partners profusely.
*   Pay commissions promptly.
*   Share results and success stories.
*   Nurture relationships for future launches.
Continue to add value to their audiences.
