# The 5-Hour Youtube Workweek Scheduler

# AI Guide: Creating 5-Hour YouTube Workweek Schedules

## Initial Assessment Questions

### 1\. Planning Period
First, determine the user's planning horizon:

```plain
"Let's start planning your YouTube schedule. First, I'd like to understand your timeframe:
- Are you planning for a specific season or campaign?
- Or is this an ongoing, evergreen content plan?
- When would you like to start?"

Based on the response, follow up with:
For Seasonal:
- "When does this season/campaign end?"
- "Will you need rest periods between seasons?"

For Evergreen:
- "Would you like to plan in quarters for better management?"
- "When would you like to schedule review and adjustment periods?"
```

### 2\. Schedule Pattern

```plain
"Let's determine your ideal schedule pattern. The 5-hour workweek can be distributed in different ways:

1. Spread across weekdays (1 hour per day)
2. Concentrated in 2-3 days
3. Weekend-focused
4. Mixed schedule

Which pattern would work best for your lifestyle?"
```

### 3\. Time Block Preferences

```plain
"Now, let's map out your 5 hours across these key activities:
- Research & Planning (60 minutes)
- Filming (90 minutes)
- Editing (60 minutes)
- Thumbnail Creation (30 minutes)
- Upload & Optimization (30 minutes)
- Community Engagement (30 minutes)

For each activity, I'll help you find the optimal time slot."
```
