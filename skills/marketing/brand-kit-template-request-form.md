# Brand Kit Template Request Form

## Brand Kit Template Request Form

**Client Information**
*   **Company Name:**
*   **Contact Person:**
*   **Email Address:**
*   **Phone Number:**
* * *

**Brand Identity Questions**
1. **Describe your business in a few sentences. What products or services do you offer?**
2. **What are your brand's core values?** (Please list 3-5 values that represent your brand.)
3. **What is your brand's mission statement?**
4. **How would you describe your brand's personality?** (Choose 3 adjectives that best describe your brand.)
5. **What emotions do you want your audience to feel when they interact with your brand?**
* * *

**Visual Identity Questions**
1. **Do you have existing brand assets?** (Please specify: logos, color palettes, fonts, etc.)
2. **What colors do you feel best represent your brand?** (If possible, include hex codes.)
3. **What fonts do you currently use or prefer for your branding?**
4. **Please upload any current logos or visual assets you have.**
* * *

**Target Audience Questions**
1. **Who is your target audience?** (Describe demographics such as age, gender, location, interests.)
2. **What problems does your product/service solve for your customers?**
3. **Who are your main competitors?** (List 2-3 competitors and what differentiates you from them.)
* * *

**Branding Preferences Questions**
1. **Are there any brands you admire? What do you like about them?** (Please list 1-3 brands and specific elements you admire.)
2. **What kind of tone or voice do you want to convey through your branding?** (E.g., professional, friendly, authoritative, casual.)
3. **Do you have any specific requirements or ideas for the brand kit template?** (Please describe any specific themes or styles you prefer.)
* * *

**Additional Information**
**Any other comments or details you'd like to share about your brand or this project?**
