# Easy Survey Script

1. **What's the title of your survey?**
    *   Example: For Sale By Owner Seller Survey; Funnel Scripts Survey; Nerve Pain Survey, etc.
2. **What's your keyword focus topic area?**
    *   Example: online business, tennis, nerve pain
3. **What is your topic? (Starts with a verb ending in "ing")**
    *   Format: "This survey is all about ______."
    *   Example: operating your business, playing tennis, suffering from pain
4. **What's the big result people want in this area? (Verb)**
    *   Format: "If you're like most people, you really want to ______."
    *   Example: get more traffic, improve your serve, lower your golf score, stop your nerve pain, etc.
5. **Basic audience identification choices**
    *   Format: "A person taking this survey could be one of the following _____."
    *   Example:
        *   male / female
        *   new author / published author / aspiring author
6. **What is a question that identifies them based on the subject of the survey?**
    *   Example: Do you have a funnel?; Have you written a book yet?; Do you need to sell without an agent?, etc.
7. **What are the answer choices for the previous subject question?**
    *   Example:
        *   Man over 30 / 40 / 50
        *   I have a working funnel / I have one but don't use it / what's a funnel?
8. **What is a question that self-declares their current level of skill/accomplishment?**
    *   Example: How much revenue does your business currently bring in per month?; How would you rate your ability at selling your books?
9. **What are the answer choices for the skill/accomplishment level question?**
    *   Example:
        *   $0-5k / $5k-$25k, etc.
        *   0-100 / 100-200, etc.
        *   I'm a newbie / I'm okay / I'm a book selling ninja!
10. **What are the answer choices for the question "What's your biggest current challenge?"**
    *   Example:
        *   I can't get enough customers
        *   My business is stuck
        *   I don't have enough money
11. **What are the answer choices for the question "Which of these 3 issues do you suffer from most?"**
    *   Example:
        *   I have no money for ads
        *   I run out of money for ads quickly
        *   I don't know how much a customer costs
12. **What is a random, seemingly unrelated question you can use to hook them at the end?**
    *   Example: Are you drinking water first thing in the morning?; Did you map out the marketing of your book BEFORE you started writing?
13. **What are the answer choices for the seemingly unrelated question?**
    *   Example:
        *   Yes
        *   No
14. **Bucket Question**
    *   Description: This is the question that identifies which landing page to send them to.
    *   Example: What is the #1 type of sales copy you need to create first?; Is your book finished and ready to start marketing?
15. **Bucket Question Answers**
    *   Example:
        *   sales letter / email sequence / ads
        *   Yes - it's done and I'm ready to sell / Yes it's done, but I don't have a sales letter / No - I need an editor / No - I need help getting it finished
16. **What will you offer them in exchange for their email?**
    *   Format: "I'm going to give them a: _____"
Example: report, video, white paper; results, etc.
