# Avatar Goals

Understanding avatar goals is crucial for taking a product to market. Know the struggles people face, their current goals, and the long-term effects. Your avatar has goals and reasons for wanting to achieve them. For instance, they might want to lose 10 pounds to improve their appearance and meet someone special or live to see their children graduate. In business, they may want to scale their ad campaign from $10K to $50K monthly for financial stability.

Every market, consumer or business, has goals and reasons for pursuing them. They also face obstacles. Consider your target market's goals and the barriers they face. Think of these barriers as bricks in a wall. Identify the three main obstacles preventing them from achieving their goals and the consequences of not overcoming them. For example, in the B2C space, if someone wants to lose 10 pounds, obstacles might include lack of time, gym intimidation, and low motivation. Failure to overcome these could lead to health risks or missed life opportunities.

In marketing, obstacles might include technical overwhelm, lack of funds for an agency, or time constraints. Not addressing these could result in business failure. The original avatar framework focuses on current pains, goals, and the consequences of inaction. We've simplified this in the app. List the top three short-term goals your avatar is trying to achieve, ensuring they relate to your product category. Consider their aspirations in your business category, whether financial advising, personal training, or marketing.

Identify the top three obstacles preventing your avatar from achieving their goals. Where can you find this information? Look at Amazon book reviews, competitor ads, blog posts, and discussions. Engage with your avatar to understand their needs. You don't need a deep-dive survey; a little research in an existing market can provide insights. Determine the main reasons your customers face challenges and their worst-case fears if these aren't addressed.
