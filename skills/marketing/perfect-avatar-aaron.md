# Target Market Matchmaker

Hey, we're starting with the Target Market Matchmaker, which is step one in the avatar sequence. It's the most important step. There are two types of people:

1. If you have an existing business, you might skip this step because you already know your target audience and the problems you solve for them. You're just looking to refine your messaging, product, and marketing strategy.
2. If you're new, considering a business model change, or starting a side hustle, this training is essential. It will help you identify who you should be helping, saving you time and effort in the future.

**Step One:** Write down who you are most passionate about helping long-term. Identify a defined group of people, known as an avatar, niche, or market segment, for whom you enjoy solving significant problems. List a few key problems you can solve for this audience. Which problem stands out as the most important? This is an initial guess, not extensive market research. You should have a general idea, like helping businesses increase sales or assisting people in losing weight.

If you have an idea or existing business, remember this is not your final answer. The process is about refinement, so document your thoughts as best as you can now. Consider the value of solving this problem for your audience. Who are they currently paying to solve it? As the saying goes, if you have a new idea or product, you’re either a fool or a genius. We want you to find a middle ground by identifying a target market with an existing, significant problem and solving it more effectively than others in the space.

Consider the potential worth of solving this problem. If it's only worth $10, scaling will be challenging. Look for a problem with substantial value because the cost of not solving it is high. Examine what people are currently paying to solve it. If there's an existing market where people are already paying, that's a positive sign. Estimate what you might charge for your program or product pathway. Determine where your target audience can be found. Are they at local events, requiring travel? Can you reach them through advertising on YouTube, Google, Facebook, or Twitter? This is not precise market research; we'll conduct that throughout the avatar process. For now, ensure there's a viable market you can reach online.

The Target Market Matchmaker is about taking action, even if imperfect. It's a preliminary look at who you should be helping—someone you're passionate about and can reach online.
