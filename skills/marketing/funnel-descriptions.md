# Funnel Descriptions

## Comparison Funnel

**Flow:**
1. **Comparison Landing Page**
2. **Detailed Comparison Page**
3. **Product-Specific Pages**
4. **Decision Helper Quiz**
5. **Recommended Product Page**
6. **Affiliate Link/Order Page**
7. **Thank You/Resource Page**

### 1\. Comparison Landing Page:
*   Overview of products being compared
*   Highlight key differences
*   Call-to-action (CTA) to view the full comparison

### 2\. Detailed Comparison Page:
*   Side-by-side feature comparison
*   Pricing breakdown
*   Pros and cons for each product

### 3\. Product-Specific Pages:
*   In-depth analysis of each product
*   User reviews
*   Expert insights

### 4\. Decision Helper Quiz:
*   Questions to assess user needs
*   Product recommendation algorithm

### 5\. Recommended Product Page:
*   Explanation of why it's the best fit
*   Key benefits for the user
*   Exclusive offer or bonus

### 6\. Affiliate Link/Order Page:
*   Clear CTA to purchase
*   Additional bonuses or guarantees
*   Secure checkout process

### 7\. Thank You/Resource Page:
*   Purchase confirmation (if applicable)
*   Additional resources for product use
*   Related product recommendations
