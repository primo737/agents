Let’s get into the 23 types of sales funnels.
1\. Squeeze Page Funnel
The Squeeze Page Funnel is the most basic type of sales funnel.
It is typically used to capture leads and build an email list.
A Squeeze Page Funnel consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where visitors can
sign up to receive your free offer.
Once they sign up, they are redirected to a thank you page where they can download
the free offer.
The Squeeze Page Funnel is a very effective way to grow your email list and is often used
as the first step in a larger sales funnel.

The Reverse Squeeze Page Funnel is similar to the Squeeze Page Funnel, but with a twist.
Instead of offering a freebie in exchange for an email address, you offer a low-priced
