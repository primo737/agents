# 23 Types Of Sales Funnel

Sales funnels are a crucial part of any successful business. They allow you to convert leads into customers by guiding them through a series of steps that increase their commitment and interest in your product or service.

But not all sales funnels are created equal - there is an array of different types that can be used depending on the needs of your business.

## Funnel Categories

In ClickFunnels Cookbook, Russell Brunson categorizes funnels into 5 categories:
- **Lead Generation Funnels** - For capturing leads
- **Buyer Funnels** - For increasing sales
- **Event Funnels** - For webinars and live events
- **Other Funnels** - Specialized purpose funnels
- **Follow Up Funnels** - For nurturing relationships

If you need to get more leads, pick a funnel type from lead generation funnels. If you want to increase sales, pick a funnel type from the buyer funnel category.

## 1. Squeeze Page Funnel

The Squeeze Page Funnel is the most basic type of sales funnel. It is typically used to capture leads and build an email list.

A Squeeze Page Funnel consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where visitors can sign up to receive your free offer. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a thank you page where they can download the free offer.

The Squeeze Page Funnel is a very effective way to grow your email list and is often used as the first step in a larger sales funnel.

---

## 2. Reverse Squeeze Page Funnel

The Reverse Squeeze Page Funnel is similar to the Squeeze Page Funnel, but with a twist. Instead of offering a freebie in exchange for an email address, you offer a low-priced product.

The key to making this funnel work is to offer a high-quality product that is undervalued. This will entice people to buy the product even though it is not free.

---

## 3. Lead Magnet Funnel

The Lead Magnet Funnel is similar to the Squeeze Page Funnel, but with a twist. Instead of offering a freebie in exchange for an email address, you offer a low-priced product.

The key to making this funnel work is to offer a high-quality product that is undervalued. This will entice people to give their email in exchange for the product.

---

## 4. Bridge Funnel

The Bridge Funnel is used to bridge the gap between two products. For example, if you have a free product and a paid product, you can use the Bridge Funnel to upsell your customers from the free product to the paid product.

The Bridge Funnel typically consists of a landing page with a video that explains the benefits of the paid product. There is also an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive more information about the paid product.

---

## 5. Survey Funnel

The Survey Funnel is used to collect data from your customers. It typically consists of a landing page with a survey.

The answers to the survey are used to segment your customers into different groups. This information is then used to create custom sales funnels for each customer group.

---

## 6. Application Funnel

Not all leads are equal. Some leads are more qualified than others.

The Application Funnel is used to qualify leads and ensure that they are a good fit for your product or service. It typically consists of a landing page with an application form.

The answers to the application are used to determine whether or not the lead is qualified.

---

## 7. Ask Campaign Funnel

The Ask Campaign Funnel is used to generate reviews and testimonials from your customers.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive your free offer. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a thank you page where they can leave a review or testimonial.

---

## 8. 2-Step Tripwire Funnel

The 2-Step Tripwire Funnel is used to sell a low-priced product.

2-Step Tripwire Funnel is designed to capture the leads information in case they drop out at the checkout. This is a great way to follow up with the potential buyer using the abandoned cart sequence.

Russell Brunson's books is a great example for 2-Step Tripwire Funnel.

---

## 9. Video Sales Letter Funnel

The Video Sales Letter Funnel is used to sell a high-priced product. It typically consists of a landing page with a video sales letter.

The video sales letter is used to convince people to buy the product.

If you don't know what funnel to build at first, start with the Video Sales Letter Funnel.

---

## 10. Sales Letter Funnel

Very similar to Video Sales Letter Funnel, the Sales Letter Funnel is used to sell a high-priced product.

The difference is that instead of using a video, you use a written sales letter.

---

## 11. Membership Funnel

The Membership Funnel is used to create a recurring revenue stream.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up for your free trial. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the membership.

---

## 12. Daily Deal Funnel

The Daily Deal Funnel is used to sell a product or service at a discounted price.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive your daily deal. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the product or service at the discounted price.

---

## 13. Invisible Funnel

The Invisible Funnel allows people to attend an event and pay only if they like it.

This is a powerful way to reduce friction and let the value speak for itself. Two Comma Club Live is an example of an Invisible Funnel where attendees can join virtually and pay only if they find value.

---

## 14. Webinar Funnel

The Webinar Funnel is used to sell a product or service and build your authority.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up for your webinar. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the product or service.

The key to webinar success: provide value first, then sell. Your attendees can see right through it if you're just trying to sell them something.

---

## 15. Automated Webinar Funnel

When you find a winner in your webinars, it makes sense to automate it and sell without you being there.

The structures and scripts are pretty much the same as a regular webinar. The difference is that you will be using an evergreen webinar platform like:
- WebinarFuel
- EverWebinar
- GotoWebinar

---

## 16. Product Launch Funnel

A product launch is a series of events leading up to the launch of a new product.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive updates about the product launch. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the product.

Jeff Walker created the Product Launch Formula which is now the standard for launching products.

---

## 17. Hero Funnel

Hero Funnel is like an invitation card for others to enter your entrepreneur world.

The goal of a Hero Funnel is to establish yourself as an authority in your industry. It starts by telling the Hero's journey - the transformation of a character from an ordinary person to a hero.

The Hero Funnel typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive your freebies. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy your products or services.

---

## 18. Homepage Funnel

The Homepage Funnel is the most basic of all funnels.

It typically consists of a landing page that leads to a product or service. It's very much the same as a traditional website but with a focus on conversions.

The goal of a Homepage Funnel is to get people to take action on your offer. A great way to capture leads on your homepage is to offer a freebie in exchange for their email address.

---

## 19. Cancellation Funnel

The Cancellation Funnel is used to stop people from canceling their subscription or membership.

It typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive your freebies. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy your products or services.

---

## 20. Storefront Funnel

Storefront Funnel is like a directory of all your products and services on a single landing page.

It is designed to illustrate everything you have to offer in one place. The goal of a Storefront Funnel is to get people to take action on your offers. They will be sent into your sales funnel where they will be given the option to buy your products or services.

---

## 21. Summit Funnel

A summit is an online event where experts in a particular field come together to share their knowledge.

The goal of a summit is to provide value and build relationships with potential customers.

A Summit Funnel typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive updates about the summit. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the summit.

---

## 22. Live Demo Funnel

A Live Demo is a great way to showcase your products or services in action. It's also a great way to build relationships with potential customers.

The goal of a Live Demo is to get people to take action on your offer.

A Live Demo typically consists of a landing page with an opt-in form where people can sign up to receive updates about the demo. Once they sign up, they are redirected to a sales page where they can buy the product or service.

---

## 23. Follow Up Funnel

The Follow-Up Funnel is what Russell describes as the most important funnel of all.

The goal of a Follow Up Funnel is to keep in touch with your customers and build relationships with them. Follow Up Funnel AKA email marketing campaign.

The most important part of the Follow Up Funnel is the email sequence. Email sequences are a series of emails that are sent out over a period of time. They are designed to build relationships with your customers and get them to take action on your offers.

Email marketing is one of the most powerful marketing strategies because it allows you to stay in touch with your customers on a regular basis. It's also one of the most cost-effective marketing strategies because it's so easy to automate.

If you're not using email marketing, you're missing out on a lot of potential customers. And that's why the Follow-Up Funnel is so important.

## Conclusion

There you have it - 23 types of sales funnels that you can use to grow your business.

Which one will you use first?

Depending on your goal, you will need to choose a different type of funnel:
- For capturing leads: use a lead capture funnel
- For increasing sales: use a sales funnel
- For building authority: use a webinar or hero funnel
- For recurring revenue: use a membership funnel

The only way to succeed with online marketing is to "Get In The Game"! And the only way to take action is to have a plan. A sales funnel is that plan.
