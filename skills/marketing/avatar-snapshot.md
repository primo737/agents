# Avatar Snapshot

The Avatar Snapshot is the next crucial step. Many advanced marketers and agency owners often overlook this. By adopting a beginner’s mindset, they achieve better results. This isn't the glamorous part of marketing, but it's essential. Understanding customer demographics, psychographics, income, and beliefs is vital. In the original Fletcher Method training, the avatar framework hasn't changed much because the fundamental motivations and drivers remain the same across industries.

Demographics include name, gender, age, location, income, and marital status. Psychographics cover activities, patterns, and beliefs. Knowing who your audience follows, the groups they subscribe to, and their interests helps in marketing. By understanding these aspects upfront, every piece of content and ad will be more effective.

Determine where your avatar spends time—online or offline. Are they on LinkedIn, Reddit, or Facebook? We'll delve into audience insights and tools later, but for now, have an idea of where your avatar is being marketed to. You might be your own avatar, knowing who's advertising to you and where. Or, your business experience might provide this insight. Consider the outcome first; this is the initial step.

The framework is straightforward. Describe your target audience in one or two sentences. For example: "Jim is an entrepreneurial executive earning X per year. He enjoys Y and struggles with Z." This statement is your mission statement. Prove you know your avatar by detailing their demographic and psychographic attributes. Understand their motivations, beliefs, and hobbies. Identify where they spend time and how to reach them effectively.
