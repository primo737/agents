# Podcast & FB Live Wizard

1. **Who will host the interview and ask the questions?**
    *   Format: "The host's name is ____."
    *   Example: Jim Edwards, Russell Brunson, Jeff Herring, Daniel Hall, etc.
2. **What is the name/title of this podcast or FB Live?**
    *   Format: "The title of the podcast/FB live show is ____."
    *   Example: I Gotta Tell You Podcast, Custom Content Insiders' Circle Podcast, Funnel Hacker Radio, Funnel Scripts Tips, etc.
3. **Who is the person being interviewed on this podcast/FB live episode?**
    *   Format: "The name of the person being interviewed is ____."
    *   Example: Armand Morin, Stew Smith, Dan Roam, etc.
4. **Target Audience for this interview (Singular)**
    *   Format: "The target audience for this interview is a/an ____."
    *   Example: Entrepreneur, Fitness Coach, Business Owner, Fitness Tester, etc.
5. **Target Audience for this interview (Plural)**
    *   Format: "____ are the target audience for this interview."
    *   Example: Entrepreneurs, Fitness Coaches, Business Owners, Fitness Testers, etc.
6. **Episode Theme: What is the focus for this interview in 2-3 words?**
    *   Format: "In a nutshell, this interview is about ____."
    *   Example: public speaking, content marketing, fitness testing, podcasting, public domain content, online business, freelance bookkeeping, etc.
7. **Interview Topic: What is the main thrust or topic of this interview?**
    *   Format: "This interview will focus on ____."
    *   Example: using public speaking to get more business; getting more traffic to your website; how Jim went from bankrupt to making his first million; getting ready for your next PT test; etc.
8. **What is the #1 biggest benefit the audience really wants from this interview?**
    *   Format: "The audience really wants to (VERB) ____."
    *   Example: get more speaking gigs; get more traffic; do more pushups; get more subscribers; etc.
9. **What is the 2nd biggest benefit the audience really wants from this interview?**
    *   Format: "The audience also wants to (VERB) ____."
    *   Example: make more money speaking; get more sales; do more pullups; get more downloads; etc.
10. **What is the 3rd biggest benefit the audience really wants from this interview?**
    *   Format: "The audience also really wants to (VERB) ____."
    *   Example: sell more from the stage; get more shares and likes; do better on timed runs; increase online visibility, etc.
11. **What is the great big "how to" payoff of the interview for the audience?**
    *   Format: "The audience really wants to know (how to) ____." **Include "how to" in the answer.**
    *   Example: how to succeed in online business; how to use public speaking to get more business; how to get more coaching clients; how to ace your fitness test; etc.
12. **What's something incredibly cool you'll drop on them during the interview?**
    *   Format: "On this interview we'll also reveal ____."
    *   Example: a cool app to help you get booked more often; how to automate your social media marketing in 5 clicks; how to get ready for a PT test in just 2 weeks; etc.
13. **What about the expert is relevant and interesting to your target audience?**
    *   Format: List relevant education, experience, awards & recognition, accomplishments, fun facts, obstacles overcome, etc.
    *   Example:
        *   Graduate of US Naval Academy
        *   Trained thousands of Navy Midshipmen
        *   Full-time fitness writer and blogger
14. **Where do you want people to go after the interview?**
    *   Format: "For more info, check out ____."
    *   Example: Jim and Jeff's New Custom Content Insiders' Circle at CustomContentInsidersCircle.com; Stew Smith's New Book "Fitness Mistakes" available on StewSmith.com, etc.
15. **What is the title of this interview?**
    *   Note: Copy and paste the title later from the Show Title Templates output.
    *   TIP: Use the "Show Title Templates" output to brainstorm titles, or copy and paste the title from your specific interview output.
16. **What points will you/do you cover in the interview?**
    *   Note: Copy and paste questions/show bullets later.
TIP: Just copy and paste or type in the exact questions later once you've created a specific interview and/or use the "Show Bullets" output for that specific interview.
