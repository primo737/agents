# Digital Marketing Onboarding Skill

This skill guides you through a complete digital marketing onboarding process for any
business. The process has 11 sequential steps. Each step must be completed and confirmed
by the user before moving to the next.

## How This Skill Works

Follow the steps below in order. At each step:
1. Explain the objective to the user
2. Ask the relevant questions or present the relevant framework
3. Collect and organize their answers
4. Present the completed deliverable for that step
5. Get explicit confirmation before proceeding

If a user asks about a specific step (e.g., "help me build an offer stack"), orient them
within the overall process but focus on their requested step. You can work on individual
steps standalone, but always reference where it fits in the bigger picture.

---

## Step 1: Clarity Call (Client Intake)

**Objective:** Understand the client's business, goals, and current situation.

Read `references/onboarding-questions.md` for the full list of intake questions.

Ask the 15 intake questions conversationally — don't dump them all at once. Group them
into 3-4 natural batches:

**Batch 1 — Business Overview (Q1-4):**
Background, USP, desired outcome, core/flagship offering.

**Batch 2 — Metrics & Traffic (Q5-9):**
Pricing, traffic sources, traffic volume, leads per month, customers per month.

**Batch 3 — Customer & Market (Q10-13):**
Current customer profile, ideal customer, "wow" factor, 2-minute pitch.

**Batch 4 — Core Desires (Q14-15):**
Prospect's #1 desire, biggest obstacle to their desired result.

After collecting answers, also determine the **Core Content elements**
(read `references/core-content-template.md`):

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Niche | The specific market segment |
| Target Audience | Who exactly the product/service serves |
| Promised Result | The specific outcome the customer gets |
| Timeframe | How long to achieve the result |
| Unique Method | What makes the approach different |
| Proof | Evidence that the method works |
| Story | The origin/transformation story |

Present a summary of all findings for confirmation before proceeding.

## Step 2: Market Research & Persona Development

**Objective:** Create 3 customer personas.

Read `references/customer-avatar-guide.md` for the complete avatar worksheet framework.

Build three distinct personas:

### 2a. Buyer Persona
The ideal customer who will purchase. Fill out all avatar fields:
- Demographics (age, gender, marital status, children, location, occupation, income, education)
- Quote (a statement that captures their mindset)
- Goals and Values
- Sources of Information (books, magazines, blogs, conferences, gurus)
- Challenges & Pain Points
- Objections to the Sale
- Role in Purchase Process

Use the "But no one else would" trick for Sources of Information — find niche interests
that specifically identify this persona and exclude everyone else.

### 2b. Amplifier Persona
People who can amplify the brand — PR contacts, influencers, partners, affiliates.
Use the same avatar framework but focus on their motivations for promoting/sharing.

### 2c. Attention Persona
The audience paying attention to brand content but not yet buying.
Focus on where they hang out, what content they consume, and what would move them
toward becoming a buyer.

Present all three personas for confirmation.

## Step 3: Customer Value Journey

**Objective:** Map the client's customer journey using Ryan Deiss' 8-stage model.

For each stage, define specific actions, content, and metrics:

1. **Awareness** — How will prospects first discover the brand?
2. **Engagement** — What content or interaction hooks their interest?
3. **Subscription** — What offer gets them to opt in (give contact info)?
4. **Conversion** — What turns a subscriber into a paying customer?
5. **Excitement** — How do you deliver a remarkable first experience?
6. **Ascension** — How do you move customers to higher-value offers?
7. **Advocacy** — How do you turn happy customers into advocates?
8. **Promotion** — How do you turn advocates into active promoters?

For each stage, specify:
- The specific tactic or touchpoint
- The content or offer used
- The success metric
- The transition trigger to the next stage

Present the complete journey map for confirmation.

## Step 4: Statement of Value

**Objective:** Craft a clear, compelling value statement.

Format: **"The [product name] enables [target audience] to [specific outcome] by [unique method] in [timeframe]."**

This should incorporate elements from the Core Content (Step 1). Create 2-3 variations
and let the user choose or refine.

Present for confirmation.

---

## Step 5: Value Ladder & Core Offer

**Objective:** Define the complete value ladder from entry point to premium offer.

Structure the ladder with these tiers:

| Tier | Purpose | Price Range | Example |
|------|---------|-------------|---------|
| Lead Magnet | Attract & capture leads | Free | Guide, checklist, quiz |
| Tripwire | Convert leads to buyers | $1-$47 | Mini-course, template pack |
| Core Offer | Main revenue driver | $47-$997 | Full course, service package |
| Profit Maximizer | Increase customer value | $997-$10K+ | Coaching, done-for-you, mastermind |

For each tier, define:
- The specific product/service
- The price point
- The key promise
- How it leads to the next tier

Include upsells, downsells, cross-sells, and any recurring billing components.

Present the complete value ladder for confirmation.

## Step 6: Offer Stack Creation

**Objective:** Build a compelling offer stack for the core offer.

Read `references/offer-stack-guide.md` for the complete offer stack creation process.

Walk through these sub-steps:

### 6a. Main Offer Overview
- Overall offer name/title
- Total value of everything included
- Special offer price

### 6b. Core Components (2-4 pieces)
For each component:
- Name/title
- Type (training, software, template, etc.)
- Individual value
- Three benefit statements:
  - "This makes it easy for you to..." (task/action)
  - "This makes it so you can..." (immediate benefit)
  - "This means you will finally be able to..." (transformation)

### 6c. Bonus Components (2-3 bonuses)
Same format as core components.

### 6d. Value Summary
Compile the full stack:
```
Here's Exactly What You're Going To Get With [Offer Name]

[Component 1] ($XXX Value)
[Component 2] ($XXX Value)
...

Act Now And Get These Incredible Bonuses...

[Bonus 1] ($XXX Value)
[Bonus 2] ($XXX Value)
...

Total Value: $X,XXX
But today, you're getting all of this...
FOR ONLY: $XXX
```

Present both a conversational version and a succinct bullet-point version for confirmation.

## Step 7: Funnel Mapping

**Objective:** Select and map the optimal funnel type.

Read `references/funnel-types-overview.md` for the 23 funnel types and when to use each.
Read `references/funnel-structures.md` for detailed page-by-page funnel structures.

### Selection Process

1. Identify the primary goal:
   - **Lead Generation?** → Squeeze Page, Lead Magnet, Survey, or Quiz Funnel
   - **Selling Products?** → VSL, Sales Letter, Tripwire, E-commerce, Free+Shipping, or Product Launch Funnel
   - **Selling Services?** → Application, Coaching, Consultant, or High-Ticket Funnel
   - **Events?** → Webinar, Automated Webinar, Summit, Live Event, or Live Demo Funnel
   - **Recurring Revenue?** → Membership, Subscription Box, or SaaS Funnel
   - **Affiliate?** → Bridge, Affiliate Marketing, Affiliate Webinar, or Comparison Funnel
   - **Retention?** → Cancellation, Follow-Up, or Challenge Funnel

2. Consider the client's:
   - Price point (low-ticket vs. high-ticket)
   - Content format preference (video vs. written vs. live)
   - Technical capability
   - Available resources

3. Recommend 1-2 funnel types with rationale.

4. Map the selected funnel page by page using the detailed structure from
   `references/funnel-structures.md`.

Present the complete funnel map for confirmation.

## Step 8: Lead Magnet Creation

**Objective:** Design the lead magnet that feeds the funnel.

Based on the chosen funnel and buyer persona, recommend a lead magnet format:
- PDF Guide / Checklist / Cheat Sheet
- Video Training / Mini-Course
- Quiz / Assessment
- Webinar / Workshop
- Template / Swipe File
- Free Tool / Calculator
- Case Study / Report

For the selected format, define:
- Title (benefit-driven)
- Core content outline
- Delivery method
- Follow-up trigger (what happens after they opt in)

The lead magnet must directly address one of the buyer persona's key pain points
and naturally lead toward the core offer.

Present for confirmation.

---

## Step 9: Follow-Up Sequences

**Objective:** Design email and SMS follow-up sequences.

Create sequences for each stage of the funnel:

### 9a. Welcome/Indoctrination Sequence (Post Opt-in)
- Email 1: Deliver lead magnet + set expectations
- Email 2: Origin story / why you do what you do
- Email 3: Value content related to lead magnet
- Email 4: Social proof / case study
- Email 5: Transition to offer

### 9b. Soap Opera Sequence (Engagement)
A story-driven email series that builds emotional connection and leads to the offer.
Each email ends on a "cliffhanger" that compels opening the next.

### 9c. Abandoned Cart / Reminder Sequence
For prospects who started but didn't complete purchase:
- Reminder 1: "Did you forget something?" (1 hour)
- Reminder 2: Address top objection (24 hours)
- Reminder 3: Scarcity/urgency (48 hours)

### 9d. Post-Purchase Sequence
- Confirmation + excitement building
- Onboarding / getting started
- Check-in + support
- Request for review/testimonial
- Cross-sell / upsell introduction

For each email, provide: subject line, purpose, key message, and CTA.

Present all sequences for confirmation.

## Step 10: Landing Page Design

**Objective:** Design the landing page(s) using the 16-point checklist.

Read `references/landing-page-checklist.md` for the complete checklist.

Every landing page must pass these 16 checks:

1. Market Callout — Visitor knows they're in the right place
2. Clear and Concise — Single message, single offer
3. Easily Understood — Passes the 5-second test
4. Compelling Headline — Clear, concise, benefit-rich
5. CTA Above the Fold — Action available without scrolling
6. Contrasting Button Color — Button stands out from design
7. Custom Button Text — Specific command, not "Submit"
8. Social Proof — Testimonials, logos, subscriber counts
9. Limited Navigation — Minimal distractions
10. Visual Cues — Arrows, boxes directing to CTA
11. Hero Shot — Image/graphic of the lead magnet or product
12. Limited Form Fields — Only ask for what you need
13. Source Congruency — Matches the ad/creative that brought them
14. Brand Consistency — Consistent look and feel
15. Enable Sharing — Social share buttons visible
16. Privacy Policy & TOS — Visible and linked

For each page in the funnel, create:
- Headline and sub-headline
- Body copy outline
- CTA button text
- Form fields needed
- Social proof elements to include
- Layout wireframe description

Use the funnel structure from Step 7 to determine which pages need to be designed.

Present the landing page specs for confirmation.

---

## Step 11: Retargeting & Tracking

**Objective:** Set up retargeting workflows and tracking.

Define:

### 11a. Tracking Setup
- Pixels to install (Facebook, Google, etc.)
- UTM parameter strategy
- Conversion events to track
- Analytics dashboards needed

### 11b. Retargeting Audiences
- Visited landing page but didn't opt in
- Opted in but didn't purchase
- Visited sales page but didn't buy
- Past customers (for upsells)
- Lookalike audiences from customers

### 11c. Retargeting Ad Sequences
For each audience, define:
- Ad creative direction
- Message/angle
- Duration
- Budget allocation

### 11d. KPIs & Reporting
- Cost per lead
- Cost per acquisition
- Conversion rates at each funnel stage
- Customer lifetime value
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)

Present the complete retargeting and tracking plan for confirmation.

## Completion

After all 11 steps are confirmed, compile everything into a comprehensive
**Digital Marketing Strategy Document** that includes all deliverables from each step.

This document serves as the complete onboarding package and implementation roadmap.
