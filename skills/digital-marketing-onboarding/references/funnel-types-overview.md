## Quick Selection Matrix

| Your Goal | Best Funnel Types |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Build email list | Squeeze Page, Lead Magnet, Quiz |
| Sell low-ticket ($1-$47) | 2-Step Tripwire, Free+Shipping, Daily Deal |
| Sell mid-ticket ($47-$997) | VSL, Sales Letter, Product Launch |
| Sell high-ticket ($997+) | Application, Webinar, High-Ticket, Coaching |
| Recurring revenue | Membership, Subscription Box, SaaS |
| Qualify leads | Survey, Application, Quiz |
| Build authority | Hero, Summit, Webinar |
| Sell for others (affiliate) | Bridge, Affiliate Marketing, Affiliate Webinar, Comparison |
| Retain customers | Cancellation, Follow-Up, Challenge |
| Launch a product | Product Launch, Waitlist, Crowdfunding |
| Sell services | Consultant, Coaching, Live Demo |
| Sell physical products | E-commerce, Storefront, Free+Shipping |

## Lead Generation Funnels

### 1. Squeeze Page Funnel
Most basic funnel. Landing page with opt-in form → Thank you page with download.
Best for: Growing email list fast with a simple free offer.

### 2. Reverse Squeeze Page Funnel
Like Squeeze Page but offers a low-priced product instead of a freebie.
Best for: Building a list of buyers (not just freebie seekers).

### 3. Lead Magnet Funnel
Opt-in page → Thank you page → Delivery page → Follow-up emails.
Best for: Capturing leads with a high-value free resource.

### 4. Survey Funnel
Landing page with survey → Segmented results → Custom funnel per segment.
Best for: Segmenting leads and personalizing the sales process.

### 5. Quiz Funnel
Quiz landing page → Questions → Email capture → Personalized results → Offer.
Best for: Engaging leads interactively and providing personalized recommendations.

## Buyer Funnels

### 6. 2-Step Tripwire Funnel
Low-priced offer → Captures lead info at step 1 → Payment at step 2.
Best for: Converting leads to buyers with a low barrier; enables abandoned cart follow-up.

### 7. Video Sales Letter (VSL) Funnel
VSL page with video → Order form → Upsell → Thank you.
Best for: Selling products $47-$2,000 with a compelling video presentation.

### 8. Sales Letter Funnel
Long-form written sales page → Order form → Upsell → Thank you.
Best for: Same as VSL but using written copy instead of video.

### 9. Product Launch Funnel
Pre-launch content series → Launch video → Sales page → Order form.
Best for: Building anticipation and urgency for a new product.

### 10. Free + Shipping Funnel
Free product offer → Order form (shipping only) → Upsell → Thank you.
Best for: Getting buyers into the ecosystem with minimal risk.

### 11. Membership Funnel
Landing page → Pricing page → Order form → Welcome → Member area.
Best for: Creating recurring revenue through subscription access.

### 12. Daily Deal Funnel
Discounted offer → Opt-in → Sales page with limited-time pricing.
Best for: Creating urgency with time-limited deals.

### 13. Invisible Funnel
Attend/consume first → Pay only if you liked it.
Best for: Reducing purchase risk and building trust.

### 14. E-commerce Funnel
Product page → Cart → Checkout → Upsell/cross-sell → Confirmation.
Best for: Selling physical products online.

### 15. Tripwire Funnel
Low-price offer page → Order form → Upsell → Downsell → Confirmation.
Best for: Converting cold leads into buyers with an irresistible small offer.

## Event Funnels

### 16. Webinar Funnel
Registration → Thank you → Live webinar → Replay → Offer → Order form.
Best for: Selling $297-$2,000+ products through live presentation.

### 17. Automated Webinar Funnel
Registration → Pre-recorded webinar → Offer → Order form.
Best for: Scaling a proven webinar without being live.

### 18. Summit Funnel
Registration → Speaker pages → Sessions → All-access pass offer → Order form.
Best for: Building authority by assembling industry experts.

### 19. Live Demo Funnel
Registration → Live demonstration → Offer → Order form.
Best for: Showcasing products/services in action.

### 20. Live Event Funnel
Event landing page → Ticket selection → Registration → Pre-event → Post-event offer.
Best for: Selling tickets and maximizing in-person event revenue.

## Other Funnels

### 21. Bridge Funnel
Landing page with video → Explains paid product benefits → Upsell from free to paid.
Best for: Bridging the gap between a free offer and a paid product (great for affiliates).

### 22. Application Funnel
Landing page → Application form → Qualification → Strategy session.
Best for: Qualifying leads for high-ticket offers ($3,000+).

### 23. Hero Funnel
Hero's journey story → Opt-in → Product/service offer.
Best for: Establishing authority through your transformation story.

## Follow-Up Funnels

### The Follow-Up Funnel (Email Marketing)
The most important funnel — keeping in touch with leads and customers through
automated email sequences. Every other funnel should feed into a follow-up funnel.

### Cancellation Funnel
Intercepts customers trying to cancel subscriptions with retention offers.

### Ask Campaign Funnel
Generates reviews and testimonials from existing customers.

## Funnel Selection Decision Tree

```
START → What's your primary goal?
│
├─ Generate Leads
│  ├─ Simple list building → Squeeze Page Funnel
│  ├─ High-value resource → Lead Magnet Funnel
│  ├─ Segment audience → Survey or Quiz Funnel
│  └─ Qualify for high-ticket → Application Funnel
│
├─ Sell Products
│  ├─ Under $47 → Tripwire or Free+Shipping
│  ├─ $47-$997 → VSL or Sales Letter
│  ├─ $997+ → Webinar or Application
│  └─ Physical products → E-commerce or Storefront
│
├─ Sell Services
│  ├─ Coaching/Consulting → Coaching or Consultant Funnel
│  ├─ High-ticket services → High-Ticket Funnel
│  └─ SaaS → SaaS Funnel
│
├─ Build Authority → Hero, Summit, or Webinar
│
├─ Launch Something New → Product Launch or Waitlist
│
└─ Retain Customers → Cancellation or Challenge Funnel
```
