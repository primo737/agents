# Conditional Operations Supplement

Use only for services that need client operations, community/events, or sensitive-service coordination. Ask only applicable questions and record the answer, approval owner, and unresolved item. Psychosocial safety is one example; adapt the domain-specific questions to the client's actual service and jurisdiction.

## Client operations and onboarding

1. What service is approved, and what is excluded?
2. Who is the executive sponsor, day-to-day contact, and final approval owner?
3. What inputs, documents, systems, permissions, milestones, and completion standard are required?
4. What response cadence is promised, and which items may be drafted, sent, or only prepared for review?
5. Who owns billing, scope changes, technical issues, and escalations?
6. Which operational KPIs matter?

## Community and events

1. What audience, outcome, format, frequency, time zone, capacity, and next action apply?
2. Does the coordinator host/moderate only, or teach/facilitate content?
3. Who owns content, run sheet, speakers, technical setup, registration, consent, recording, accessibility, and moderation?
4. What pre-event/post-event communications and metrics are approved?
5. What is the live-event escalation path?

## Sensitive services, including psychosocial safety work

1. What employer, jurisdiction, worksite, approved offer, and delivery boundary are in scope?
2. Who are the executive sponsor, People/HR or WHS lead, qualified specialist, EAP/crisis contact, and escalation owner?
3. Which claims, client materials, workshops, and reports are approved?
4. What minimum information is needed, and what privacy, consent, confidentiality, retention, and access rules apply?
5. What outreach segment/channel/volume is permitted, and who approves it?
6. What must be escalated for clinical, worker-distress, urgent-risk, legal, WHS, privacy, or commercial handling?

## Boundary

Operations staff may coordinate approved work, records, communications, and event logistics. They do not independently provide clinical, counselling, legal, or WHS advice; determine compliance or risk controls; promise outcomes; quote unapproved terms; or disclose sensitive employee information.
