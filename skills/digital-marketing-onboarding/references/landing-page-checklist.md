# 16-Point Landing Page Checklist

Every landing page in the funnel must pass all 16 checks before launch.

## The Checklist

### 1. Market Callout
Your visitor needs to know they're in the right place. Call out to them either
directly (e.g., "Attention: [target audience]") or indirectly with recognizable
images and vocabulary that resonate with the target market.

### 2. Clear and Concise
The best landing pages have a single message and make a single offer. Make sure
your landing page isn't trying to do too much. One page = one goal.

### 3. Easily Understood
If a visitor can't figure out what you're offering in 5 seconds or less, you'll
lose them. Perform the 5-second test: show it to someone unfamiliar for 5 seconds,
then ask them what it's about.

### 4. Compelling Headline
You need a clear, concise, benefit-rich headline that grabs attention and tells
visitors they've come to the right place. Lead with the promised result.

### 5. CTA Above the Fold
Most visitors won't scroll below the fold. If you're making a free offer, give
them a chance to take action without scrolling.

### 6. Contrasting Button Color
The button color should contrast (NOT blend in) with surrounding design elements.
It needs to visually pop and draw the eye.

### 7. Custom Button Text
"Submit" is not good enough. Use button text that gives a specific command or
speaks to the end result. Examples:
- "Get Free Instant Access"
- "Download My Free Guide"
- "Start My Free Trial"
- "Show Me My Results"
- "Yes! Send Me the [Resource]"

### 8. Social Proof
Include elements that show visitors they're making a smart decision:
- Social share counts
- "As seen on" logos
- Testimonials with names and photos
- Number of downloads or subscribers
- Star ratings or reviews

### 9. Limited Navigation
Remove or minimize navigation elements. The landing page should have one clear
path — toward the CTA. No menus, no footer links to distract.

### 10. Visual Cues
Incorporate arrows, boxes, highlighted areas, and other visual devices to draw
the eye to the call-to-action area.

### 11. Hero Shot
Include an image or graphical representation of the lead magnet or product.
This typically bumps conversions, but test with and without. Start with it as
the control.

### 12. Limited Form Fields
Don't ask for information you don't need. If you only plan to follow up via
email, just ask for name and email at most. Consider testing without the name
field if you don't personalize follow-up messages.

### 13. Source Congruency
The text and imagery on the landing page should match (ideally exactly) the
text and imagery from whatever ad or creative brought the visitor there.
Mismatched messaging = high bounce rates.

### 14. Brand Consistency
You don't have to put your logo on every landing page, but the overall look
and feel should be consistent with your core brand — colors, fonts, tone.

### 15. Enable Sharing
While landing pages don't typically go viral, some visitors will click Facebook
and Twitter share buttons. Make it easy and obvious for them.

### 16. Visible Privacy Policy and TOS
Not only are privacy policies and terms of service required to advertise on some
platforms (including Google), they're also good for conversions — they signal
trustworthiness.

## Landing Page Design Template

For each landing page in the funnel, document:

```
PAGE: [Page Name]
PURPOSE: [What this page should accomplish]

HEADLINE: [Benefit-rich headline]
SUB-HEADLINE: [Supporting statement]

BODY COPY OUTLINE:
- [Key point 1]
- [Key point 2]
- [Key point 3]

CTA BUTTON TEXT: [Action-oriented text]
BUTTON COLOR: [Contrasting color choice]

FORM FIELDS: [List only what's needed]

SOCIAL PROOF: [Specific elements to include]

HERO IMAGE: [Description of image/graphic]

CHECKLIST COMPLIANCE:
□ Market Callout    □ Clear & Concise    □ 5-Second Test
□ Compelling Headline □ CTA Above Fold   □ Contrasting Button
□ Custom Button Text  □ Social Proof     □ Limited Navigation
□ Visual Cues        □ Hero Shot         □ Limited Fields
□ Source Congruency  □ Brand Consistency  □ Sharing Enabled
□ Privacy/TOS
```
