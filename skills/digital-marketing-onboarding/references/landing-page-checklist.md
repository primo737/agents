# Landing-page review

Use applicable checks for the page's goal, audience, channel, and jurisdiction. Mark each as pass, change, unknown, or not applicable. This is a review aid, not a universal conversion formula.

- Is the audience and next action clear from the entry context and headline?
- Do the headline, offer, and claims match the source ad or link and approved offer?
- Is the call to action understandable and usable on relevant devices and assistive technology?
- Are forms limited to information needed for the next step, with appropriate privacy notice and permissions?
- Is the claimed proof authentic, current, relevant, and permitted for use? If none exists, avoid placeholder testimonials or logos.
- Are pricing, terms, availability, delivery boundaries, and any limitations accurately stated?
- Does the page work at representative viewport sizes, load reliably, and provide a usable error/success state?
- Are tracking and attribution configured only as authorized and permitted for this audience and location?
- Does the path after submission or payment actually deliver the promised next step?

A hero image, above-the-fold CTA, minimal navigation, social sharing, and visual cue can help in some designs; none is automatically required. Validate design choices against the actual audience and outcome rather than claiming a universal conversion lift.
