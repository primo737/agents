# Offer composition guide

Use when an offer has multiple meaningful components. An offer stack is optional; a simple service may be clearer with one concise scope and price.

Record the core product or service, delivery format, included components, exclusions, support, timing, capacity, price or pricing logic, and the approval owner. For each component, explain the customer benefit and the evidence supporting any measurable claim. Add bonuses only if they are real, deliverable, and useful to the audience.

Do not assign invented standalone dollar values to components, imply a savings calculation without a real comparison, or manufacture urgency, scarcity, a guarantee, testimonial, or regulatory assurance. Mark proposed terms as proposals until approved. If a deadline or capacity limit exists, document its source and operational reason.

A reviewable offer brief includes the promise, actual inclusions, delivery boundary, price status, verified proof, unresolved decisions, and the next action for the client.
