# Adaptable funnel structures

Use these as sketches, not page-by-page requirements. Omit steps that do not help the audience or business. For each retained step, specify its purpose, owner, input, output, permission or approval, and success measure. Do not imply results, scarcity, testimonials, or guarantees that have not been verified.

## Direct offer

1. A relevant source introduces the problem and approved offer.
2. An offer page or equivalent conversation explains scope, price or pricing process, proof, limits, and next action.
3. Enquiry or checkout captures only necessary data and gives clear confirmation.
4. Delivery or sales handoff is tested end to end.

## Consultative service

1. A service explanation helps the buyer judge fit and what is outside scope.
2. An enquiry form or scheduling step gathers information needed for an informed call.
3. A qualified owner reviews and responds within the stated process.
4. Proposal, agreement, onboarding, and follow-up follow actual approval and delivery boundaries.

## Resource-led education

1. A resource solves a relevant, bounded question.
2. Access is direct or uses a justified opt-in with clear expectations.
3. Follow-up, if permitted, helps the person take the next useful step.
4. The paid offer appears only when the connection is clear and accurate.

## Event or demo

1. An invitation states audience, value, format, date/timezone, access, and what happens afterward.
2. Registration collects only needed details and provides confirmation.
3. Reminder and attendance handling follow the user's channel permissions.
4. The event or demo leads to a relevant next step, with a clear owner for questions and escalation.

## Commerce or subscription

1. Product or plan details clarify inclusions, price, billing, availability, delivery, and support.
2. Checkout captures required information and handles errors, consent, and payment safely.
3. Confirmation reflects the actual transaction and gives fulfilment or account access.
4. Post-purchase communication supports use, service, renewal, or cancellation as applicable.

## Review before implementation

Check that source and destination messages match; claims and proof have sources; forms, tracking, and audience use are permitted; mobile and accessibility needs are met; and submission, payment, notifications, and fulfilment work in a representative end-to-end test. Record unknowns and owners rather than silently assuming completion.
