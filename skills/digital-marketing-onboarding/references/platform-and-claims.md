# Platform, data, and claims checks

Reviewed 2026-09-28. This is a decision checklist, not a substitute for current platform terms or legal review. Recheck the actual jurisdiction, platform, account, and campaign before implementation.

- **First-party audiences:** Establish who collected the data, for what purpose, whether the client can direct its use, and whether required notices, permissions, and consent are in place. Google Customer Match and Meta Customer List Custom Audiences have platform-specific requirements; neither a file's availability nor a generic consent label establishes permission. See [Google Customer Match policy](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6299717?hl=en-audiences) and [Meta Custom Audiences Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/customaudience/update).
- **Tracking and retargeting:** Check the applicable privacy rules, platform policy, account eligibility, and geography before specifying pixels, conversion tracking, or remarketing. Google describes consent requirements for data collection and remarketing; [Customer Match consent signals](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14546648?hl=en) have additional regional conditions.
- **Sensitive targeting:** Check category restrictions before defining audiences. For example, [Google's personalized advertising policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/143465?hl=en) limits certain demographic targeting for housing, employment, and consumer finance ads in the US and Canada.
- **Claims and social proof:** Use sourced claims and authentic customer words. Verify permissions, relevant outcomes, and material connections. The [US FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/topics/truth-advertising/advertisement-endorsements) is one jurisdiction-specific example; requirements elsewhere may differ.
- **Operational record:** Record the source or evidence, permitted use, owner, date checked, remaining question, and approval required before live use. If a source is unavailable, mark the claim or targeting step unverified and use a viable alternative.
