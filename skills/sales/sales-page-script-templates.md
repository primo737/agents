# Sales Page Script Template (Continued)

1. **What is something you were struggling with at the time?**
    *   Format: "I was struggling with _______."
    *   (Examples: finding a buyer for two houses I owned; selling from the stage without being pushy; my pushup counts as well as my run times, etc.)
2. **As a result, what negative thing, emotion, or circumstance was putting your back up against the wall at the time?**
    *   Format: "I was getting more and more _______."
    *   (Examples: frustrated and scared; sick, frustrated and behind on my bills; disgusted with myself and my crappy fitness level, etc.)
3. **What's something bad that happened/occurred at this time that threw you off track?**
    *   Format: "Then the bottom fell out from under me when _______."
    *   (Examples: I had to get there in 30 days or they'd give the job to someone else; I was stranded in FL with no money; my CO told me I had 30 days to get squared away, etc.)
4. **What is the thing you really needed to be able to do, but couldn't, when the event in the previous question happened?**
    *   Format: "Which meant I couldn't _______."
    *   (Examples: give my new family the life I promised them; even pay for a taxi to get home from the airport; keep my job if I didn't raise my score fast, etc.)
5. **What is the #1 thing your audience does NOT know how to do yet, but want to?**
    *   Format: "Do you know how to _______?" (VERB)
    *   (Examples: get your home sold fast and for top dollar; get paid from the stage FAST; be ready for and ace every PT test anytime, etc.)
6. **What is the first thing everyone tries to get results that doesn't work?**
    *   Format: "So first I tried _______, and that didn't work!"
    *   (Examples: sticking a sign in the yard; running ads on Facebook; killing myself in the weight room, etc.)
7. **What is something everyone tries to get results that REALLY doesn't work?**
    *   Format: "And I EVEN tried _______, and that was a total disaster!"
    *   (Examples: holding an open house every Saturday for a month; speaking for free at the local Chamber of Commerce; wrapping myself in plastic wrap from the kitchen pantry, etc.)
8. **What is the NEXT thing everyone tries to get results that works a little bit, but still sucks?**
    *   Format: "So then I tried _______, and that worked a little bit!"
    *   (Examples: running an ad in the paper; hiring a speaker bureau; one of those fad diet supplements you see on TV, etc.)
9. **What is the thing, method, etc. you discovered that changed it all?**
    *   Format: "And that's when I finally tried _______ and it worked!"
    *   (Examples: leveraging social media to find a buyer; using a FREE book to fill my speaking gig funnel; periodization training and muscle confusion to kickstart my muscles, etc.)
10. **Who is the enemy that's sabotaging everyone's results in this area?**
    *   Format: Can be people or circumstances. (PLURAL) "The real enemy here are _______!"
    *   (Examples: Realtors; speaker bureaus; BS personal trainers; traditional, outdated ideas about training for a test, etc.)
11. **What is the big lie the enemy tells that everyone believes?**
    *   Format: "They want you to believe _______, but that's BS!"
    *   (Examples: you can't sell without an agent because you need the MLS; you can't get good speaking gigs without an expensive promoter; it takes months to get ready for a PT test, etc.)
12. **What's the big revelation you had in this area?**
    *   Format: "Then I finally figured out that _______, and everything changed!"
    *   (Examples: I could use social media to sell my house fast; I could use my own free book to get all the speaking gigs I could handle; I could get ready for a PT test in less than 2 weeks; etc.)

# Sales Page Script Template (Continued)

1. **What did this big revelation reveal to you?**
    *   Format: "I discovered how to _______." (starts with a VERB)
    *   (Examples: get other people to send me all the buyer leads I wanted; turn my book into a 24/7 "speaker booking machine"; drop my run times without logging 100's of painful miles, etc.)
2. **What's the 2nd big thing you figured out from this big revelation?**
    *   Format: "I also discovered how to _______." (starts with a VERB)
    *   (Examples: get those buyers to compete for my house (NOT me begging them to buy); create sales messages that made promoters to beg me to speak; increase my pushups without injury, etc.)
3. **What's the 3rd big thing you figured out from this big revelation?**
    *   Format: "I even discovered how to _______." (starts with a VERB)
    *   (Examples: structure the deal so it was a "win-win" for all parties; build my back-end funnel so I made MORE money speaking; keep myself ready for any PT test at a moment's notice, etc.)
4. **How long did it take to figure all this out?**
    *   Format: "This took _______ (TIME) to figure out and create."
    *   (Examples: years of blood, sweat and tears; months of focused effort; weeks of trial and error; many sleepless nights, etc.)
5. **How much did it cost to figure all this out?**
    *   Format: "This cost _______ (MONEY) to figure out and create."
    *   (Examples: over $100,000; tens-of-thousands of dollars in development cost; a whole month's salary, etc.)
6. **What's the biggest roadblock you (and they) had to overcome in this area?**
    *   Format: "The biggest thing holding me back was _______." VERB ending in "ing"
    *   (Examples: finding a qualified buyer for your house; getting booked for profitable speaking gigs; getting ready for a fast-approaching PT test, etc.)
7. **How long did it take to get results the OLD way?**
    *   Format: "It used to take me _______ to get results the old way."
    *   (Examples: at least 2 months; weeks of struggle; months of torture; days of mind-numbing effort, etc.)
8. **How long does it take to get results with this NEW way?**
    *   Format: "With this NEW way, now I get results _______."
    *   (Examples: in just a few minutes; in less than 15 minutes; whenever I want; in just 2 weeks or less; 100X faster, etc.)
9. **What's the #1 FEATURE (what it is) of your product, service, software, etc.?**
    *   (Examples: learn to write great classified ads; create the ultimate speaker one-sheet; emergency fitness training routine, etc.)
10. **What is the BENEFIT (what it does/means) of that #1 feature?**
    *   Format: "With feature #1 you can _______." (Starts with a VERB)
    *   (Examples: generate tons of buyer leads this weekend; instantly set yourself apart from every other speaker they're considering; reach passing levels for your test in a month or less, etc.)
11. **What's the #2 FEATURE (what it is) of your product, service, software, etc.?**
    *   (Examples: negotiate win-win contracts with nervous buyers; learn to create the ultimate presentation to sell; know what to eat and drink before, during, and after the fitness test, etc.)
12. **What is the BENEFIT (what it does/means) of that #2 feature?**
    *   Format: "Feature #2 means you can _______." (Starts with a VERB)
    *   (Examples: avoid having a deal blow up in your face; make a ton of sales no matter how resistant the audience; instantly have the best energy levels for high performance, etc.)
