# Star, Story, Solution Wizard

1. **What is the name of what you're selling?**
    *   (Examples: Funnel Scripts, Click Funnels, Super Keyword Scraper, Selling Your Home Alone, Speaker Goldmine, PT Test Success, etc.)
2. **What exactly are you selling?**
    *   (Examples: Software, Home Study Course, Book, Executive Coaching, Done-for-you Service, etc.)
3. **Your name**
    *   (Examples: Jim Edwards, Russell Brunson, etc.)
4. **Keyword Theme: In 2-3 words, what is the area of focus for this product, service, etc.?**
    *   Format: "In a nutshell, this product, service, etc. is focused on the area of ________."
    *   (Examples: public speaking, content marketing, fitness testing, podcasting, public domain content, online business, freelance bookkeeping, etc.)
5. **What is the #1 thing the audience really wants related to the product, service, etc. topic?**
    *   Start with a VERB
    *   Word Suggestions: (Examples: get more speaking gigs; get more traffic; do more pushups; get more subscribers; increase market share, etc.)
6. **What is the 2nd thing the audience really wants related to the product, service, etc. topic?**
    *   Start with a VERB
    *   Word Suggestions: (Examples: make more money speaking; get more sales; do more pullups; get more downloads; create a steady stream of innovative new ideas, etc.)
7. **What is the 3rd thing the audience really wants related to the product, service, etc. topic?**
    *   Start with a VERB
    *   Word Suggestions: (Examples: sell more from the stage; get more shares and likes; do better on timed runs; increase your online visibility; stand out as the leader in your market, etc.)
8. **What is an emotional payoff your target audience really wants to HAVE?**
    *   Start with a VERB
    *   (Examples: sell a house without an agent; speak in front of huge paid audiences; ace any PT test like a boss, etc.)
9. **What is an emotional payoff your target audience really wants to GET RID OF?**
    *   Start with a VERB
    *   (Examples: tell your real estate broker to kiss off; stop speaking to small, uninterested audiences; stop struggling to barely pass your PT test every 6 months, etc.)
10. **What is the #1 big HOW TO your audience really wants?**
    *   Format: "My audience really wants to know (HOW TO) _______."
    *   (Examples: how to market your home like a pro; how to get paid to speak; how to become a PT god; how to lose weight fast and keep it off, etc.)
11. **What's a negative event, circumstance, etc. you want to build your story around?**
    *   Format: "Let me tell you a quick story about _______."
    *   (Examples: the time I had to sell 2 houses myself in 30 days; when I got laughed off stage because I was so bad; how I failed my PT test, etc.)
12. **What is something you were trying to do/accomplish at the time?**
    *   Format: "At the time, I was trying to _______."
    *   (Examples: move to Florida to take a big promotion; build my speaking business; make a great impression at my new unit and get a promotion, etc.)
