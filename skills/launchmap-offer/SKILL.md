## The Offer Ecosystem

The Perfect Offer isn't one product — it's a complete ecosystem that meets the avatar at every level of readiness and investment.

---

## Exercise 1: The Profit Pyramid

Four phases, bottom to top:

**Phase 1 — Free (Lead Generation)**
Free content, lead magnets, opt-ins. The goal is to get them into your world.
Ask: "What free content do you currently give away? Or what could you give away that would attract your avatar?"

**Phase 2 — Low Ticket ($7–$97)**
Entry-level paid products. Buyers list. Separates the curious from the committed.
Ask: "Do you have a low-cost product? Or what's the simplest version of your methodology you could package for under $100?"

**Phase 3 — Core Offer ($500–$5,000)**
The main product. This is what the MDM is selling. This is where the transformation happens.
Ask: "What is your core offer — your primary program or service?"

**Phase 4 — High Ticket ($5,000+)**
Mastermind, done-for-you, retainer, private coaching. For those who want the fastest result with the most access.
Ask: "Do you have or want a high-ticket or VIP option? What would that look like?"

Map out their current ecosystem. Identify gaps. Note which phase they're strongest in and which is missing.

## Exercise 3: Delivery Checklist

Determine HOW the offer is delivered:

Ask each question and note the answer:

- **Format:** Live coaching, group program, self-paced course, done-for-you, hybrid?
- **Duration:** How long does the program run? Weeks, months?
- **Access:** What does the client get access to? (Recordings, community, 1:1 calls, resources?)
- **Frequency:** How often do they interact with you? Weekly calls? Daily check-ins?
- **Support:** How do they get help between sessions?
- **Guarantee:** Is there a result guarantee or refund policy?

Note any delivery gaps — things the avatar will expect that aren't yet in the offer.
