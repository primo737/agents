# The Perfect Webinar Script

This is the master presentation that sells. The structure is always the same:
**hook the audience, tell your story, break their false beliefs with three
"secrets," then transition into the offer and stack the value.** Everything
before the offer exists to dismantle the three reasons the audience would say no:
they don't believe the *vehicle* works (Secret #1), they don't believe *they*
can do it (Secret #2), and they don't believe their *circumstances* allow it
(Secret #3). Handle those three and the sale becomes easy.

When you build a webinar with someone, you're filling in this skeleton with their
specifics. Walk them through it section by section. For the three secrets, the
key move is to surface the false belief the audience holds, tell a quick story or
show proof that shatters it, and replace it with a new empowering belief ("The
truth is…").

## Section A — Introduction (set the hook + remove blame)

- Open by removing blame: if they've failed at [topic] before, it's not their
  fault. Put their past fears to rest.
- Name a common enemy (the big corporation / guru / old way) that wanted them to
  believe they couldn't succeed.
- Promise the one big result of the webinar (the "big domino" — the single belief
  that, if they accept it, makes the sale inevitable).

**Your goal statement (say it twice, slightly differently):**
> I want to help [the people who want RESULT A] and [the people who want RESULT
> B]… without [the pain/struggle they fear].

**The "only way" bridge:**
> The ONLY WAY to get [big result] is through [the vehicle/category]… and the
> ONLY WAY to do *that* is through [your specific product].

## Section B — Who you are + Origin Story

- Quick credibility: who you are, why they should listen (keep it humble — "not a
  technical person," "almost went broke three times," "figured it out the hard
  way").
- Then tell the **Origin Story** (build it with `references/origin-story.md`).
- "How does this apply to you?" — works no matter what they sell, makes possible
  what wasn't, anyone can do it.
- **Results / Proof** — at least one case study by name.

## Section C — Set up the three secrets

Tell them exactly what's coming. Each secret is framed as a curiosity-driven
"how to" promise:

> - Secret #1: [How to overcome the vehicle objection]
> - Secret #2: [How to overcome the "I can't do it" objection]
> - Secret #3: [How to overcome the "my situation is different" objection]

## Section D — The three secrets (repeat this mini-structure for each)

For **each** secret, walk through the same five beats:

1. **"Some people think…"** — state the false belief out loud, then "let me tell
   you a quick story…"
2. **Background** — the story of how you discovered the truth.
3. **Examples / Results / Case Studies** — proof: stats, screenshots, students.
4. **"You might also be thinking…"** — the secondary objections, listed
   ("…work in my market," "…work if I sell physical products," etc.).
5. **The Truth** — the new empowering belief that replaces the false one.

(See the worked example below for the exact wording of all three secrets.)

## Section E — Transition to the offer

- Trial close: "Are you enjoying the webinar so far?"
- Recap "We've covered…" the three secrets.
- "So who wants to take things to the next level? Put '[I'm ready!]' in the
  chat." — this gets micro-commitments before the pitch.

## Section F — Introduce the offer + build the stack

This is the heart of the close. You introduce the core offer, then add each
component one at a time, and **after every addition you re-show the entire stack
with running values.** This "stack slide" repetition is what makes the final
price feel small. For each component use the same three-beat script:

1. **The struggle/cost** — "I had to spend months / tens of thousands to figure
   this out… but you won't have to, because I'm giving you [component]."
2. **The benefit + how** — "This makes it faster/easier to [result]. How? Because
   [mechanism]."
3. **Objection handle** — "You might be thinking you can't use this because [X].
   That's not true because [Y]." Then state the **value** of that component.

After each component, show the growing "What You're Going To Get" list with
dollar values, building toward the Total Value.

**Then the price reveal sequence:**
- "If all it did was…" (justify the price three ways — each "if all it did was X,
  would it be worth [anchor price]?").
- "I had two choices: make it cheap, or make it valuable." → you chose valuable.
- Anchor high ("$20,000 value… regular price…"), then reveal the real price.
- **Guarantee** — money-back if it doesn't deliver the core promise.
- **The real question** — "Is it worth gambling a few minutes to check this out?
  Even if it only does HALF of what I claimed, it pays for itself."

## Section G — The bonus (urgency)

Add a fast-action bonus, usually scarcity-limited ("First 50 only"), using the
same three-beat component script. Then re-show the full stack one final time with
the bonus included and the Total Value.

## Section H — Get started now + the closes

- Final "Get Started Now" stack slide with the URL and the offer summary.
- Then deploy a handful of **the 16 closes** (see `references/16-closes.md`).
  Don't use all 16 — pick the 4–6 that best strengthen the specific argument and
  audience. Always end on the "Close Close."

## Worked example — the ClickFunnels "Funnel Hacks" webinar

Use this end-to-end example to show the user what a finished script looks like.
Title: **"How To Set Up a 7-Figure Funnel in 30 Minutes Without Being Held
Hostage By a Tech Guy."**

**Intro / blame removal:** "If you've failed at sales funnels before, it's not
your fault. Big corporations want you to think you can't set up profitable
marketing funnels without them — but it's not true."

**Goal:** "I want to help people who want to increase sales and people who want
to set up profitable marketing funnels… without getting bogged down in technical
stuff or being held hostage by a tech guy. The ONLY WAY to do that is through
sales funnels — and the only way to do *that* is through ClickFunnels."

**Origin story:** broke student athlete, newly married, selling potato guns,
Google raised ad rates, figured out funnels, it took huge amounts of time, built
the tooling, now living the dream. **Proof:** Trey Lewellyn case study.

**The three secrets:**
- **Secret #1:** How To Ethically "Steal" Over $1,000,000 Worth of Funnel Hacks
  From Your Competitors For Under $100. *(Truth: the sales-funnel process works
  to sell ANYTHING.)*
- **Secret #2:** How To Then Clone Their PROVEN Funnel (Inside ClickFunnels) In
  Less Than 10 Minutes. *(Truth: ClickFunnels does all the work for you — you
  don't need to be a programmer or designer.)*
- **Secret #3:** How To Get The Exact SAME Customers Going To Your Competitors'
  Funnels To Start Coming To Yours Instead. *(Truth: you can see where
  competitors get their traffic and run ads there too.)*

**The offer — "Million Dollar Funnel Blueprint":** help you set up profitable
funnels in 30 minutes without being held hostage by a tech guy.

The stack (built one component at a time, re-shown after each):
- 6 Week Funnel Hacks Masterclass — $997
- ClickFunnels: 6 Months Enterprise Account — $3,564
- Million Dollar Funnel Clones — $997
- Copywriting Secrets Course — $997
- Instant Traffic Hacks — $1,997
- BONUS: Unlimited Funnels In Your Account (First 50 Only) — PRICELESS
- **Total Value: $20,000** → special price **$1,997**

**Who it works for:** authors, ecommerce sellers, newsletter publishers, software
publishers, membership owners. **#1 reason people don't start:** "I don't have a
product" → start with affiliate products, plus we show you how to make one in a
couple hours.

This example uses funnels/ClickFunnels, but the *structure* is the product. Swap
in the user's topic, vehicle, story, three beliefs, and stack components, and the
machine works for any market.
