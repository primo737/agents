# Live Event Invitation & Email Promo Sequence

This is the promotion engine that *fills* the webinar or live event once the
registration page exists. It produces an opt-in page, social posts, "stealth
closes" (soft PS-style invitations you drop into other emails), and a full timed
email sequence from announcement through the day-of last call. Use it when the
user says they've built the webinar/registration page and now need to drive
traffic and registrations.

Run it as Jim: gather the 22 variables a handful at a time, acknowledge each,
read them back at the end, then generate the assets the user asks for. Replace the
placeholder promoter name throughout with the user's actual name.

## The 22 variables

1. Event
2. Event Type
3. Event Title
4. Event Tagline
5. Your Name
6. 2–3 word topic
7. Date
8. Time
9. Top problem (the audience's)
10. #1 thing they want from this event
11. #1 fear
12. "Big picture" payoff
13. Benefit #1
14. Benefit #2
15. Benefit #3
16. Cool secret
17.–21. Five tips / tricks / "what you'll discover" points
22. Specific payoff (the see-exactly-what-it-takes outcome)

## Email sequence

Send across the run-up to the event. Keep subject lines curiosity-driven and the
body short — the only job of each email is the click to register.

**Email — 3 to 7 days before**
> Subject: [#3] (webinar)
> Hi #NAME#, [Your Name] here with an exclusive free webinar invitation…
> This [#7] at [#8] I'm hosting a special webinar that will [#12]: "[#3]".
> How would you like to [#13]? Want to [#14]? We're delivering that and much
> more — plus you'll see exactly what it takes to [#22].
> Attendance is LIMITED — register now: [LINK]
> P.S. Sign up now and we'll see you on [#7] at [#8]: [LINK]

**Announcement email (long or short)**
> Subject: "[#3]" Webinar
> Hi #NAME#, [Your Name] here. Next week we're hosting "[#3]" — [#4].
> DATE: [#7]  TIME: [#8]
> At this free webinar you'll [#12]. You can [#13], [#14], and even [#15]!
> (Long version adds the five [#17]–[#21] bullets.)
> Claim your spot: [LINK]

**Email — 1 day before** (three flavors: salesy / low-key long / low-key short)
> Subject: Re: #NAME#, are we still on for tomorrow?
> Hi #NAME#, Can you really [#13]? [#14]? [#15]? YES — you can. And this [#7]
> we'll help you do exactly that on our free webinar. Seating is LIMITED —
> register now: [LINK]

**Email — day of** (salesy / low-key long / low-key short)
> Subject: It's TODAY at [#8]
> Hi #NAME#, today is our free webinar "[#3]". If you want to [#13], [#14], and
> even [#15], you need to be there. It starts at [#8] — time's running out to
> register. Don't procrastinate and kick yourself later: [LINK]

Offer the user whichever pieces they need rather than dumping all of it. A typical
launch uses: opt-in page + 3 social posts + the 3-7-days, 1-day, and day-of
emails.
