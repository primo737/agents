# Opt-In, Registration & Landing Page Copy

This covers everything that gets people *onto* the webinar (or onto your list):
the registration page, the opt-in video script, and squeeze pages for lead
magnets. The job of every one of these is narrow and ruthless: promise one big
result, tease the curiosity gaps, and get the email. Don't sell the product here
— sell the *click*.

There are two generators below. Use the **Webinar Opt-In generator** for a
registration page that drives webinar signups. Use the **Lead Capture generator**
for a squeeze page that delivers a free report / lead magnet. Ask the user which
they need (or build both).

---

## Webinar registration page (filled example structure)

A clean registration page has: the webinar title as headline, date/time, a short
description, 3 curiosity bullets (one per webinar secret), and a register button.

> **NEW Webinar: "[Webinar Title]"**
> Date: ___  Time: ___
>
> On this FREE webinar we'll show you how to [big result in a sentence].
> So if you want to [result A] and [result B] without [the pain], you need to
> attend this breakthrough event.
>
> Here's just part of what we'll reveal:
> - [Secret #1 promise] — *([the truth/payoff in parentheses])*
> - [Secret #2 promise] — *([payoff])*
> - [Secret #3 promise] — *([payoff])*
>
> …and much, MUCH more!
> Don't miss this breakthrough webinar that will finally get you [the result].

## Opt-in video script (for the registration page)

A 30–45 second video lifts opt-in rates. Two interchangeable openers:

> **Version A:** Do you want to [result A]? Are you tired of [pain]? Hi, my name
> is [Name] and I've got a free webinar for you titled "[Title]" that's going to
> show you how to [big result]. So if you want to [result A] and [result B]
> without [pain], you need to attend this breakthrough event! Just fill out the
> form on this page and you'll be registered instantly. We only have a limited
> number of seats and these always fill up fast — so don't get left behind,
> register now.
>
> **Version B:** (same skeleton, lead with result B and the opposite pain.)

## Webinar Opt-In generator (11 variables)

Ask these, then assemble the video script and on-page text below.

1. Your Name
2. Webinar Title
3. Target niche audience (singular — e.g. "public speaker")
4. Big payoff they want (starts with a verb — e.g. "make more money speaking")
5. Big "how to" secret they want (include the words "how to")
6. Pain they want to avoid (verb ending in "-ing" — e.g. "begging for gigs")
7–11. The five points you'll cover on the webinar

**Video script:**
> Attention [#3]s who want to [#4]. You're about to discover the secret of [#5]
> without the pain of [#6]. Hi, my name is [#1] and I'd like to invite you to a
> very special webinar event titled: "[#2]". On this training you'll discover:
> - [#7]
> - [#8]
> - [#9]
> - [#10]
> - [#11]
>
> So if you're serious about wanting to [#4] without [#6], register now for this
> special event that shows you exactly [#5]. Register Now!

**On-page text:** same headline + bullets, plus a scarcity line: "WARNING: Space
is limited and these LIVE trainings always fill up, because they're significantly
better than information others charge thousands for — even though they're free."

## Lead Capture generator — squeeze page for a free report (8 variables)

Use this when the offer is a free lead magnet rather than a webinar.

1. Name
2. Website
3. Niche group (target audience)
4. Big result they want (starts with a verb)
5. Big "how to" secret they want to know
6. Bribe for opt-in (e.g. "Free Report")
7. Big prize/payoff of the free bribe
8. Big takeaway/reveal on the next (sales) page

**Squeeze page video/audio script:**
> Hi, my name is [#1] and welcome to [#2]. In the next few minutes you're going
> to discover how to [#4], just like [#3]s all over the country. We've put
> together a [#6] showing you [#7]. Just enter your first name and email and
> click the button below, and you'll instantly receive the [#6] by return email.
> On the next page you'll get all the details on how to [#8]. So go ahead — enter
> your name and email and click the button to get your [#6] now.

**On-page bullets (the three-bullet promise):**
> - [#4]
> - [#7]
> - [#8]
>
> **YES! I'm ready to discover [#5] and [#4]! Please send my [#6] now.**
> [ Send My Free Report Now ]
> *We respect your email privacy.*

## Landing page sales copy notes

When the user wants longer landing-page sales copy (not just a squeeze page),
build it from the same raw materials in a logical order: attention-grabbing
headline (the biggest promise + the removed pain), a subhead that names the
audience, the origin story (condensed), the three curiosity bullets expanded into
short paragraphs, proof/case studies, the offer, and a strong call to action
repeated at least twice. Headlines almost always follow the pattern **"How To
[Big Result] Without [The Pain]"** — that single formula does a lot of the heavy
lifting, so start there.
